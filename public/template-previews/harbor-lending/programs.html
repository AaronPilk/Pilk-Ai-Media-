Loan programs

Every route to the water, mapped

Six proven programs, one calm process. Tell us where you're headed and we'll chart the way — whether that's a first home near the boardwalk or a jumbo loan on the bluff.

Most popular

Conventional

The dependable standard for primary residences — and a strong fit for well-qualified second-home buyers along the coast.

  • Down payments as low as 3% for qualified buyers
  • Fixed and adjustable rate options, 10–30 year terms
  • Second-home financing with as little as 10% down
  • No mortgage insurance with 20% or more down
Start with Conventional
Flexible credit

FHA

A friendly entry point when your credit history has a few barnacles or your savings are still growing.

  • Down payments from 3.5%
  • Credit scores from 580 considered
  • Gift funds allowed for the down payment
  • Competitive rates backed by federal insurance
Start with FHA
For those who served

VA

Earned benefits for veterans, active-duty service members, and eligible surviving spouses. Coastal duty stations welcome.

  • 0% down payment for eligible borrowers
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • Limits on closing costs you can be charged
  • Reusable benefit — use it more than once
Start with VA
Higher balances

Jumbo

Built for waterfront and view properties that sail past standard loan limits — including luxury second homes and select vacation-rental purchases.

  • Loan amounts above conforming limits, into the millions
  • Primary, second-home, and vacation-property options
  • Interest-only and ARM structures available
  • Underwriting that understands complex income
Start with Jumbo

  • Free side-by-side comparison before you commit
