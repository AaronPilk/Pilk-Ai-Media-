About ClearPath

We started this company because mortgages were confusing on purpose.

The short version.

ClearPath was founded after our team spent a combined two decades inside big retail lenders — watching good borrowers get steered into whatever product paid best that quarter, buried in disclosures nobody explained.

So we built the brokerage we wished existed. One where every quote comes with the math behind it. Where "what's the catch?" gets a straight answer. Where your advisor's job is measured by how well you understand your loan, not just whether it closes.

Today we help hundreds of families a year buy, build, and refinance — and our best marketing is still a client explaining their own mortgage to a friend, correctly, from memory.

What we won't compromise on.

01Clarity before commitment

You'll never be asked to sign anything you can't explain back to us. If a document is confusing, that's our failure — and we'll fix it before moving forward.

02The math, in writing

Every recommendation comes with a written comparison: total cost, break-even point, and the trade-offs. You keep the document whether you work with us or not.

03One advisor, start to finish

No call centers, no ticket numbers. The person who quotes your loan is the person who answers your texts the week you close.
