Lending Programs

Six instruments, chosen with care.

Each engagement begins the same way: we listen, we compare every suitable program in writing, and we recommend only what serves your interests. The programs below form the foundation of our residential practice.

PROGRAM I

Conventional Financing

The prudent standard

For borrowers of sound credit, conventional financing remains the most economical path to ownership. We attend particularly to the total cost of capital over the life of the loan — not merely the advertised rate.

  • Down payments from 3% for qualified first-time purchasers
  • Private mortgage insurance retired at 20% equity
  • Fixed terms of 15, 20, and 30 years
  • Rate comparisons across our full correspondent panel
Inquire
PROGRAM II

FHA Lending

The accessible beginning

We hold no snobbery about government-insured lending; some of our longest client relationships began with an FHA loan. It is a sensible instrument for those early in their financial lives, and we administer it with full ceremony.

  • 3.5% down with a 580 credit score or better
  • Accommodating debt-to-income guidelines
  • Family gift funds permitted for the entire down payment
  • A clear path to conventional refinancing as equity builds
Inquire
PROGRAM III

VA Financing

For those who served

The VA benefit is, in our judgment, the finest lending program in the country — and it is administered too often with carelessness. Our bankers handle the Certificate of Eligibility, the appraisal particulars, and the funding fee counsel personally.

  • No down payment required
  • No monthly mortgage insurance, in perpetuity
  • Certificate of Eligibility obtained on your behalf
  • A benefit that may be exercised more than once
Inquire
PROGRAM IV

Estate & Jumbo Lending

Our signature practice

Significant properties call for underwriting that comprehends trusts, partnership distributions, and income earned across jurisdictions. This is the work our firm was built for, and where our correspondent relationships prove most valuable.

  • Financing well beyond conforming limits
  • Down payments from 10% for exceptional files
  • Trust, LLC, and entity vesting accommodated
  • Portfolio and interest-only structures where appropriate
Inquire
PROGRAM V

Refinance Counsel

Dispassionate by design

A refinance is a mathematical question, and we treat it as one. Every proposal includes a break-even analysis in writing. When restructuring does not serve you, we advise against it — and have done so more times than we can count.

  • Rate-and-term restructuring with full cost accounting
  • Cash-out financing for improvements or consolidation
  • Written break-even analysis with every proposal
  • Streamline programs for existing FHA and VA obligations
Inquire
PROGRAM VI

First Home Counsel

The beginning of a relationship

Your first mortgage should be a tutorial, not a trial. We pair first-time purchasers with assistance programs where available, explain each document before it is signed, and remain your firm long after the closing dinner.

  • Down payment assistance program review
  • Written pre-approval, typically within two business days
  • One senior banker from first call to settlement
  • An unhurried education in the instrument you are signing
