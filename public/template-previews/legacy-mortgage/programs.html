The Programs

Six ways to finance the exceptional.

Legacy leans jumbo and portfolio by design — the loans that reward judgment over checklists. Every program below is underwritten in-house, by people whose names you will know.

Our Lead Discipline No. 01

Jumbo Lending

The heart of the house. Financing above conforming limits for primary residences, second homes, and select investment properties — priced sharply and closed quickly.

  • Loan amounts to $5M; scenarios above by exception
  • Rate locks up to 90 days, with float-down at no charge
  • As little as 10% down to $2M for qualified borrowers
  • Nine-day average clear-to-close on complete files

No. 02

Portfolio Lending

Loans we hold on our own balance sheet, which means we answer to our own judgment. Built for complex income, entity vesting, and properties the agencies decline on principle.

  • Asset-depletion and bank-statement qualification
  • Vesting in trusts, LLCs, and family partnerships
  • Interest-only structures to preserve liquidity
  • Cross-collateralization for multi-property clients

No. 03

Conventional Financing

When a conforming loan is simply the right instrument, we execute it with the same care as a $5M jumbo — and often at pricing your bank cannot match.

  • Fixed and adjustable terms from 15 to 30 years
  • Rate locks up to 60 days at no cost
  • Ideal for pieds-à-terre and family purchases
  • Same senior principal, start to finish

No. 04

Construction & Renovation

Ground-up builds and serious renovations, financed with a single closing. Your draws are administered by a dedicated principal who has read the plans — not a processing queue.

  • Single-close construction-to-permanent, to $5M

  • Draw inspections scheduled around your builder
  • Renovation lending against post-completion value

Refinance & Cash-Out

A refinance should be a balance-sheet decision, not a rate chase. We model the whole picture — carrying cost, liquidity, tax posture — before recommending a structure.

  • Equity release for acquisitions and investments
