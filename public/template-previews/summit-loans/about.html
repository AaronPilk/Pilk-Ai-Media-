About Summit

We rebuilt the mortgage from basecamp up.

The origin story.

Summit started with a simple observation: getting a mortgage felt slower than buying the house. Faxed documents in 2020. Voicemails that took days to return. Rate quotes that changed by the time you called back.

So we built a lending platform where the technology does the waiting, not you. Automated document analysis, real-time lender pricing, and milestone tracking you can check from your phone at midnight — paired with loan experts who pick up when it matters.

Four thousand seven hundred closings later, the model holds: speed and clarity aren't opposites. They're the whole point.

Our principles

Three rules we climb by.

RULE 01

Speed is respect

Your time matters. Pre-approvals in 24 hours, document reviews same-day, and questions answered before they become worries.

RULE 02

Show the whole board

Every quote comes with every option we priced — including the ones we don't recommend, and why. You choose with full information.

RULE 03

Tech plus humans, never or

Software handles the repetitive work. Licensed experts handle the judgment calls. You always know which one you're talking to.
