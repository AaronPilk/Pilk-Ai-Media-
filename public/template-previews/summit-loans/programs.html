Conventional

Most popular

The go-to for borrowers with solid credit. Flexible terms, competitive pricing, and mortgage insurance that actually goes away.

  • As little as 3% down for first-time buyers
  • PMI removable at 20% equity
  • 15, 20, and 30-year fixed terms
  • Single-family, condo, and 2-4 unit properties
Start with Conventional

FHA

Low down payment

Government-backed flexibility for buyers still building credit or savings. One of the most forgiving paths to a first home.

  • 3.5% down with 580+ credit score
  • Higher debt-to-income allowances
  • Entire down payment can be gifted
  • Streamline refi available down the road
Start with FHA

VA

For those who served

The strongest benefit in American lending, earned through service. Zero down, no monthly mortgage insurance, great rates.

  • $0 down payment required
  • No monthly mortgage insurance
  • We pull your Certificate of Eligibility
  • Reusable benefit — not one-and-done
Start with VA

Jumbo

High balance

Financing beyond conforming limits, built for complex income. Tech equity, business ownership, K-1s — we've seen it all.

  • Loan amounts into the millions

  • ARM and interest-only structures
  • Bank-statement qualification available
