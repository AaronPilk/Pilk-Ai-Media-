Our story

Mortgages felt broken. So we fixed the part that hurts.

We started SwiftApprove because getting your first mortgage felt like doing taxes in a foreign language. Forty-page documents, acronyms nobody explains, and a loan officer who calls back three days later — if at all.

So we built the lender we wished existed: real humans who text back within minutes, paperwork translated into plain English, and pre-approvals that show up in hours instead of weeks.

Fifteen thousand first homes later, we still celebrate every single closing like it's our own. Because for someone, it's the biggest day of their year — and we get to hand them the keys.
