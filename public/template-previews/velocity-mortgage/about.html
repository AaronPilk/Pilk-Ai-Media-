Our story

Built by people tired of slow closings.

We spent a decade inside big lenders watching great buyers lose houses to paperwork. So we rebuilt the mortgage from scratch — around speed, honesty, and actual human beings.

Why Velocity exists

The mortgage wasn't broken. The process was.

In 2019, our founder watched a first-time buyer lose her dream home because a big-bank underwriter took 47 days to review a file that should have taken 4. The seller walked. The buyer cried. The bank shrugged.

That week, Velocity started as a question: what if a lender underwrote your file first — before you ever made an offer — so closing was the easy part?

Five years and $1.2 billion later, that's still the whole playbook. Underwrite early. Communicate daily. Close fast. It turns out speed isn't magic — it's just refusing to let files sit.
