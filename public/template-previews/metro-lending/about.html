About Metro Lending Group

Built for buildings.

Metro started with one observation: suburban lenders keep losing city deals. Not on rates — on buildings. A condo with thin reserves, a co-op board with a hard deadline, a walk-up with a mixed-use ground floor. That's where deals die.

So we built a team that reads condo docs like underwriters, tracks board deadlines like closers, and treats a 12-day clear-to-close as the standard, not the exception.

3,400+ closings later, the thesis holds: know the building, win the deal.

How we operate

Three non-negotiables

01

Speed is respect

Same-day callbacks. Updates before you ask. In a competitive market, a slow lender costs you the unit — so we don't do slow.

02

Underwrite first

We look at the hard stuff — income, building, docs — up front. A pre-approval from Metro means something at the offer table.

03

Say the number

Rate, fees, break-even — in plain English, in writing, before you commit. If a refi doesn't pencil, we tell you to keep your loan.
