Three things your payment is telling you.

20% down kills PMI

Put down less than 20% on a conventional loan and you'll usually pay private mortgage insurance — often $100–$250/mo. Hit 20% and it vanishes from the equation.

15-year terms trade payment for interest

A 15-year loan raises the monthly payment but typically cuts your rate and saves six figures in lifetime interest on a mid-size loan. Toggle the term above and compare.

Taxes vary wildly by county

Property tax rates range from under 0.5% to over 2.3% depending on where you buy. Ask us for the actual rate on any address — we'll pull it in minutes.
