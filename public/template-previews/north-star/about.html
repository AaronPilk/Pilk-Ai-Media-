✦ Our mission

Nobody should navigate the biggest purchase of their life alone.

We exist to make the mortgage the calmest part of buying a home — with honest math, human guidance, and a fixed point to steer by.

✦ Our story

Founded by two loan officers who were tired of watching buyers get lost.

NorthStar started in 2014 with a folding table, two phones, and a simple observation: most borrowers weren't confused because mortgages are complicated. They were confused because nobody bothered to explain anything.

So we built the lender we wished existed. One loan officer per file, start to finish. Underwriting down the hall, not across the country. Weekly updates sent before you think to ask. And a standing rule that still governs every deal: if the math says wait, we say wait — even when it costs us the loan.

Eleven years and $650 million later, the folding table is gone. The rule isn't.

✦ What we steer by

Four fixed points on our compass.

✦

Honest math

Every quote itemized, every fee explained, every break-even calculated in front of you. If the numbers don't work, we'll be the first to say so.

✦

One guide per voyage

You get a single loan officer from first call to closing table. No handoffs, no call centers, no re-explaining your file to a stranger.

✦

Speed with rigor

In-house underwriting gets most files clear-to-close in 15 days — without cutting the corners that blow up at the closing table.

✦

Every door open

We lend the way the law intends: fairly, to every qualified borrower. Equal Housing Lender isn't fine print here — it's the point.
