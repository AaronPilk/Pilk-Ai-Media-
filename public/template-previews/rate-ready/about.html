About RateReady

We made mortgages move.

A lending platform built by people who got tired of watching the industry waste everyone's time.

Our story

Getting a mortgage felt stuck in 1995. So we rebuilt it.

We started RateReady because our founders went through the mortgage process themselves and couldn't believe it: fax machines, week-long silences, mystery fees that appeared at the closing table, and "rates" that required three phone calls to actually see.

Meanwhile, every other financial product had gone digital. You could open a brokerage account in three minutes — but borrowing money for the biggest purchase of your life still meant a paper mountain and a month of waiting.

So we rebuilt the whole thing from the application up. One online application. Real rates in minutes, not teaser rates in email chains. Digital documents. Proactive status updates. And a human loan expert on your side the entire way — because software should speed people up, not replace them.

Today we've funded over $450 million for more than 1,900 families, with an average close of 21 days. We're just getting started.
