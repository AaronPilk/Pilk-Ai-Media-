Three ways to shrink that number

Put more down — or don't

20% down avoids PMI, but 3–5% down programs get you in the door years sooner. The right answer is math, not a rule of thumb.

Shop the rate, not the teaser

A quarter point on a $320,000 loan is roughly $50 a month — about $18,000 over 30 years. We show real rates upfront so you can compare honestly.

Consider a shorter term

A 15-year loan carries a higher payment but a lower rate — and can cut your lifetime interest by more than half. Toggle the term above to see it live.
