Loan programs

Six honest ways to get home

Every family's path looks a little different. Here's the menu — and a real person to help you order.

First-Time Buyer

Your first home is our favorite kind of loan. We'll slow down, explain every step, and celebrate right alongside you. Nobody rushes you, and no question is too small.

  • Down payments as low as 3%
  • Credit guidance if your score needs a boost
  • Best for: buyers who want a patient guide
  • Free homebuyer prep session included
Start here
🏠

Conventional

The dependable classic. If you've got steady income and decent credit, a conventional loan usually means great rates and flexible terms with no surprises.

  • Down payments from 3–20%
  • Works for credit scores 620 and up
  • Best for: buyers with solid footing
Apply now
🛠️

FHA

A friendlier front door. FHA loans are government-backed with easier credit requirements, so a few past bumps don't have to keep you renting.

  • As little as 3.5% down
  • Credit scores from 580 welcome
  • Best for: rebuilding credit or smaller savings
Apply now
🎖️

VA

For those who served, and the families beside them. VA loans can mean zero down and no monthly mortgage insurance — a benefit you've more than earned.

  • $0 down payment for eligible veterans
  • No private mortgage insurance
  • Best for: veterans, active duty & surviving spouses
Apply now
🏝️

Jumbo

Bigger home, bigger loan — same warm treatment. When your dream house sits above conforming limits, we keep the process just as personal.

  • Loan amounts above $806,500
  • Competitive rates for strong credit
  • Best for: higher-priced homes & move-up buyers
Apply now
🔁

Refinance

Already home? Wonderful. Let's make sure your loan still fits your life — a lower rate, a shorter term, or cash out for that kitchen you've been sketching.

  • Rate & term or cash-out options
  • Side-by-side savings math, in plain English
  • Best for: current homeowners re-checking the numbers
