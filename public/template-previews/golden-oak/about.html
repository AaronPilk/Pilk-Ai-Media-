Our story

A lender that still answers the phone on Saturday.

Golden Oak began at a kitchen table, and fifteen years later it still runs like one — first names, honest numbers, and nobody left waiting for a call back.

Since 2011

Rooted in trust. Growing with you.

Founded in 2011 by Robert and Eleanor Hale, after twenty years watching big banks treat borrowers like file numbers.

The Hales started Golden Oak with a simple rule: never put a family into a loan you wouldn't put your own family into. That rule has survived rate spikes, refi booms, and $480 million in funded loans — because it works.

Today the team has grown to twelve, but the model hasn't changed. One loan officer owns your file from the first hello to the closing table. No call centers, no departments, no "let me transfer you."

We've helped more than 1,900 families buy first homes, forever homes, and everything in between — and about a third of our business each year comes from their referrals. That's the number we're proudest of.

What we stand on

Three promises, kept daily.

No. 01

Tell the whole truth

Every quote comes with the full cost picture — rate, points, fees, and the break-even math. If waiting six months is your best move, we'll say so and mean it.

No. 02

Move like it matters

A slow lender can cost you the house. Same-day pre-quals, 24-hour underwriting turns, and a 17-day average close aren't marketing lines here — they're standards.

No. 03

Be there after closing

Rates drop, families grow, plans change. We review every past client's loan annually and call only when there's real money on the table for you.
