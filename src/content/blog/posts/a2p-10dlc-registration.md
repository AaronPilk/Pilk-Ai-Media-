If your CRM's texts are not being delivered, or your provider keeps asking you to "complete A2P registration," this is why. US carriers now require businesses to register before sending texts from regular local numbers through software. It is a one-time hassle that makes automations possible, such as missed call text back, appointment reminders and lead follow-up. Here is how it works.

## What does A2P 10DLC mean?

- **A2P** means application-to-person: texts sent by software, such as your CRM, rather than typed one-by-one on a phone.
- **10DLC** means 10-digit long code: a standard local phone number, like (727) 555-0123.

Carriers created the system to cut spam. Registered business traffic gets better delivery; unregistered traffic gets filtered or blocked.

## How does A2P 10DLC registration work?

Registration happens through your texting provider, such as your CRM's phone system, and goes to The Campaign Registry, the industry database carriers use.

1. **Brand registration**: your legal business name, EIN, address, website, contact details and business type. These must match official records exactly.
2. **Campaign registration**: what kind of messages you will send, such as customer care, account notifications or marketing, with sample messages, how people opt in, and how they opt out.
3. **Carrier review**: carriers approve or reject the campaign.
4. **Number assignment**: your phone numbers are linked to the approved campaign.

Businesses without an EIN can use a limited sole proprietor option with lower sending limits.

## How long does A2P 10DLC registration take?

Brand registration can be quick. Campaign review typically takes from a few days to a few weeks, and longer if it is rejected and resubmitted. Start registration as soon as you set up a new texting number, before you need it.

## What does A2P 10DLC registration cost?

There are small one-time registration and vetting fees and monthly campaign fees, plus carrier surcharges on messages. Providers usually pass these through at cost. Check your provider's current pricing.

## Why do A2P campaigns get rejected?

The most common reasons:

- **Business details do not match** the IRS or state records exactly.
- **Website problems**: no website, a site that does not match the business, or no privacy policy.
- **Missing opt-in details**: carriers want to see how people agree to receive texts.
- **No opt-in language on forms**: your website forms should include a consent checkbox or statement.
- **Vague campaign description** or sample messages that do not match the stated use.
- **Prohibited content**, such as certain loan offers, cannabis or third-party lead generation.

## What should your website have before you register?

- Your legal business name and contact information.
- A privacy policy that says you do not share or sell mobile numbers or opt-in data for marketing by third parties.
- Terms that describe your text messaging: what messages people will get, frequency, that message and data rates may apply, and how to opt out by replying STOP and get help by replying HELP.
- Forms with clear text consent language, ideally an unchecked optional checkbox.

## How does A2P 10DLC relate to TCPA consent?

They are separate. A2P registration is a carrier requirement for delivery. TCPA is US law about when you may send automated texts and calls, especially marketing. You need both: registration so messages get delivered, and proper consent so they are lawful. See [TCPA text message consent](/blog/tcpa-text-message-consent/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need A2P 10DLC registration to text customers?

If you send texts from a standard local number through software, such as a CRM or texting app, yes. Texts typed from your personal phone are different.

### What happens if I do not register?

Carriers filter or block unregistered business texts, so your messages may never arrive, and your provider may suspend sending.

### Can I register without an EIN?

Sole proprietors without an EIN can use a limited registration option with lower sending volumes.

### Is toll-free texting an alternative?

Yes. Toll-free numbers use a separate verification process instead of 10DLC registration. Some businesses use them for high-volume messaging.
