Facebook ads can be one of the best lead sources for real estate agents, or a fast way to burn money on unqualified leads. The difference usually comes down to understanding Meta's housing rules, choosing the right offer and following up fast. Here is what works now.

## What are Meta's rules for real estate ads?

Ads about real estate sales, rentals, home insurance and mortgages fall under Meta's special ad categories. For housing ads, you cannot target by age, gender or ZIP code, lookalike audiences are not available, location targeting must cover at least a 15-mile radius around a point, and some detailed targeting options are removed. These rules exist to prevent discrimination under fair housing laws.

In practice, that means Meta decides more of who sees your ad. Your creative and offer do the targeting. We explain the categories in [Meta special ad categories](/blog/meta-special-ad-categories/).

## Which real estate ad campaigns get leads?

**1. Home valuation offers for seller leads.** "What's your home worth right now?" leading to a valuation page. Usually the best seller lead source. See [home valuation landing pages](/blog/home-valuation-landing-page/).

**2. Listing videos.** A short, well-shot video tour of a new listing reaches local buyers and shows sellers how you market homes.

**3. Neighborhood guides.** Helpful guides to living in an area attract people planning moves months ahead.

**4. Just-sold and results ads.** Proof that you sell homes in the area, aimed at future sellers.

**5. Retargeting.** Ads to people who visited your site or watched your videos, reminding them to book a call.

## What makes real estate ad creative work?

- **Real video and photos** of homes, neighborhoods and you, not stock images.
- **A clear first three seconds** that stop the scroll.
- **One message per ad**: one listing, one offer, one area.
- **Captions on videos**, since many people watch without sound.
- **Fair housing language**: describe the property and area, never the kind of person it suits.

## Should real estate ads use lead forms or landing pages?

Both work. Meta lead forms are fast and get more leads, but often lower quality. Landing pages get fewer leads with more intent and let people see more about you. Many agents use lead forms for valuation offers and landing pages for listings. See [Facebook lead ads vs landing pages](/blog/facebook-lead-ads-vs-landing-page/).

## How much should a real estate agent spend on Facebook ads?

Enough to test one offer properly for a few weeks without changing everything daily. Judge by cost per qualified conversation and closed deals over time, not cost per click. Real estate leads take time to mature, so give campaigns months, not days. Our guide to [how much Facebook ads cost](/blog/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost-small-business/) explains budgeting.

## Why do real estate ad leads go cold?

Usually because nobody follows up fast or long enough. Most online real estate leads are early in their journey. What works:

1. An instant text and email when the lead arrives.
2. A call within minutes during business hours.
3. A nurture sequence for months, with market updates and new listings.
4. Your CRM tracking every touch.

See [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/) and [GoHighLevel for real estate](/blog/gohighlevel-for-real-estate/).

## What tracking do real estate ads need?

Install the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API so Meta can see which ads produce real leads, and mark form submissions as conversions. Without this, Meta optimizes for clicks instead of leads. See [Meta Pixel vs Conversions API](/blog/meta-pixel-vs-conversions-api/).

## Should agents run ads themselves or hire help?

Run them yourself if you have time and a small budget. Get help if you are spending enough that mistakes are expensive, or if your ads keep getting rejected or producing junk leads. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can real estate agents target by ZIP code on Facebook?

No. Housing ads cannot target by ZIP code. Location targeting must use at least a 15-mile radius around a point, city or region.

### Are Facebook real estate leads any good?

They can be, but many are early in their search. Fast follow-up and long-term nurturing turn more of them into clients.

### What is the best Facebook ad for listing leads?

A short video tour of a new listing, aimed at the local area, usually performs well and doubles as marketing proof for future sellers.

### Do I need to select the housing category for every real estate ad?

Yes. Ads promoting real estate sales or rentals must run under the housing special ad category, or Meta may reject them.
