Wix is one of the most popular website builders for a reason: you can get a decent-looking site online in an afternoon without touching code. A custom website, built by a designer or developer, is a bigger investment. This guide compares the two honestly, including where Wix is the better choice.

## What is the difference between Wix and a custom website?

Wix is a hosted website builder. You pick a template, drag elements around, and pay Wix a monthly or yearly fee that covers hosting and the editor. Your site lives on Wix's platform.

A custom website is designed and built for your business, either from scratch or from a professional starting point, and hosted wherever you choose. You or your developer control the code, the design and where it runs.

## How do Wix and custom websites compare?

| | Wix | Custom website |
|---|---|---|
| Up-front cost | Low | Higher |
| Ongoing cost | Monthly plan, plus apps | Hosting and optional support |
| Who builds it | Usually you | A designer or developer |
| Design freedom | Within Wix's editor | Anything you can describe |
| Speed | Decent, harder to tune | Can be very fast |
| Moving away | You cannot export the site | You own the code and can move it |
| Integrations | Wix apps and some connections | Whatever the business needs |

## When is Wix the better choice?

Wix makes sense when:

- **Your budget is tight** and you have time to build the site yourself.
- **You need something simple**: a few pages, a contact form, maybe bookings.
- **You want to edit everything yourself** without waiting on anyone.
- **You are testing an idea** and do not want to invest heavily yet.

There is no shame in starting on Wix. Plenty of businesses do, and its SEO tools cover the basics.

## When is a custom website the better choice?

A custom site pays off when:

1. **Your website is a main source of leads.** Small gains in speed, clarity and conversion add up to real money.
2. **You need specific features**: quote calculators, CRM connections, custom forms, member areas, listings or integrations Wix does not handle well.
3. **You want to own your site outright.** Wix does not let you export your site to host it elsewhere, so leaving means rebuilding.
4. **Your brand needs to stand out** from template-built competitors.
5. **You are spending money on ads**, where the landing page decides your cost per lead.

## Is Wix bad for SEO?

No. Wix has improved a lot, and a well-written Wix site can rank for local searches. The limits show up at the edges: less control over page speed, code and structured data, and fewer options for advanced technical work. For most small businesses, content, reviews and a Google Business Profile matter more than the platform. ## Can you move from Wix to a custom website later?

Yes, but it is a rebuild, not a transfer. The content, images and domain come with you; the design and code do not. To protect your search rankings when you switch:

- Keep the same domain.
- Keep your most important page addresses, or redirect old addresses to the new ones.
- Move your best content over, improved rather than deleted.
- Resubmit your sitemap in Google Search Console.

## How do you decide between Wix and custom?

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Will I really build and maintain it myself?** If not, Wix's main advantage disappears.
2. **How much is one new customer worth?** If a few extra leads a month pays for a custom site, it is usually worth it.
3. **What will the site need to do in two years?** If the answer includes bookings, CRM, automations or custom tools, plan for that now.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I export my Wix site to another host?

No. Wix does not let you export your site's code to host elsewhere. You can take your content, images and domain, but the site itself has to be rebuilt.

### Is Wix cheaper than a custom website?

Usually at the start. Over several years, monthly plans, paid apps and your own time can narrow the gap, especially if the site needs features Wix handles poorly.

### Can a Wix site rank on Google?

Yes. Wix covers SEO basics well. Content, reviews and local listings usually matter more than the platform for small businesses.

### Do custom websites need more maintenance?

They need hosting, updates and occasional edits. A support plan covers that, or a well-built static site can need very little ongoing work.
