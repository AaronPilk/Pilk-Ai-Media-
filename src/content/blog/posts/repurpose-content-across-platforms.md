Most small businesses do not run out of ideas; they run out of time. Repurposing solves that by turning one piece of work into a week of content. The key is adapting, not copying: the same idea, shaped for each platform. Here is a simple system.

## Why repurpose content instead of creating new posts?

- **Saves time**: one filming session feeds many posts.
- **Reaches different people**: your audience on LinkedIn is not the same as on TikTok.
- **Reinforces your message**: people need to see an idea several times before it sticks.
- **Improves quality**: you can put more effort into one strong core piece.

## What makes a good core piece?

Start with something rich enough to split up:

- A short video answering a real customer question.
- A behind-the-scenes look at a project from start to finish.
- A customer story, with permission.
- A blog post or guide on your website.
- A live session or webinar recording.

Customer questions are the easiest source: every question you answer on a call is a potential post.

## How do you turn one video into a week of content?

Take a three-minute video answering "How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Tampa?":

| Platform | Format |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels and TikTok | 30 to 60 second vertical clip with captions and a strong hook |
| YouTube | Full video, plus a Short from the best moment |
| Facebook | Native video upload with a short written intro |
| LinkedIn | Written post with the three main points and one photo |
| Instagram Stories | Poll or question sticker based on the topic |
| Email | Short note summarizing the answer, linking to the full video |
| Website | Blog post or FAQ section built around the transcript |
| Google Business Profile | Update with a photo and a link |

## How should you adapt content for each platform?

- **Hook**: change the first line or first two seconds for each audience.
- **Length**: shorter for Reels and TikTok, fuller for YouTube and LinkedIn.
- **Format**: vertical video for short-form, square or landscape for feeds and YouTube.
- **Tone**: more casual on TikTok and Instagram, more professional on LinkedIn.
- **Captions**: always for video; many people watch without sound.
- **Call to action**: match the platform, such as "link in bio," "comment for details" or "book a call."

AI tools can draft captions from a transcript, suggest hooks, pull key quotes and outline a blog post from a video. Edit everything for accuracy and your voice. ## How do you schedule repurposed content?

Batch it: film once, edit the pieces in one session, and schedule them across the week with a scheduling tool or your CRM's social planner. Space versions of the same idea a few days apart on each platform. ## What repurposing mistakes should you avoid?

- Posting the identical caption and video everywhere.
- Horizontal video cropped badly into vertical formats.
- Watermarks from one app showing on another.
- Ignoring comments because the post was scheduled.
- Repurposing weak content; start with your best.

## How does repurposed content help your website and search?

Turning video answers into written pages on your site gives you content that can rank in search and be cited by AI tools, while your social posts point people back to it. See [how to show up in ChatGPT search](/blog/how-to-show-up-in-chatgpt-search/).

## What does a simple monthly repurposing plan look like?

Film four core videos in one afternoon, one per week. Turn each into a vertical clip, a written post, a Story, an email section and a website FAQ. That is roughly twenty pieces of content from one session, published steadily through the month.

## Who should own repurposing in a small business?

One person, even part-time. They collect raw footage and customer questions, cut clips, write captions and schedule posts. The owner films and approves. A clear owner keeps the system running.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it okay to post the same content on every platform?

Repurposing the same idea is fine. Adjust format, hook and caption for each platform rather than posting identical copies.

### What is the best content to repurpose?

Short videos answering real customer questions. They split easily into clips, captions, emails and website content.

### How often can I reuse old content?

Evergreen content can be reshared every few months, especially with a fresh hook or updated details.

### Do I need special tools to repurpose content?

No. A phone, a simple editing app and a scheduler are enough. AI tools can speed up captions and transcripts.
