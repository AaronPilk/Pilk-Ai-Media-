When you run Facebook or Instagram ads for leads, you have two main choices: an instant form inside the app (lead ads) or a link to your own landing page. Both work. They produce different kinds of leads, and the right choice depends on your offer, your follow-up and what a customer is worth to you.

## What is the difference between lead ads and landing pages?

**Lead ads** open a form inside Facebook or Instagram. Meta prefills details like name, email and phone from the person's profile, so submitting takes a couple of taps.

**Landing pages** send people from the ad to a page on your site or funnel, where they read more and fill out a form.

## How do lead ads and landing pages compare?

| | Lead ads | Landing page |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead | Usually lower | Usually higher |
| Lead volume | Higher | Lower |
| Lead intent | Often lower | Often higher |
| Information shown before submitting | Limited | As much as you want |
| Tracking setup | Simpler | Needs Pixel and Conversions API |
| Follow-up speed needed | Very fast | Fast |
| Reusable for other channels | No | Yes |

## When should you use lead ads?

- **Simple offers** where people do not need much information, such as a free estimate or a home valuation request.
- **Mobile-heavy audiences** who will not wait for a page to load.
- **Fast follow-up**: you can call or text within minutes, before interest fades.
- **Testing** an offer quickly.

## When should you use a landing page?

- **Higher-value services** where trust matters and people want to see reviews, photos and details.
- **Complex offers** that need explanation.
- **Lead quality problems** with lead ads.
- **Retargeting and analytics**: landing page visitors can be retargeted and studied.
- **Multiple channels**: the same page works for Google ads, email and social posts. See [landing page vs website](/blog/landing-page-vs-website/).

## How do you improve lead ad quality?

If lead ads bring in people who do not answer or do not remember filling out the form:

1. **Choose the "higher intent" form type**, which adds a review step before submitting.
2. **Add one or two qualifying questions**, such as timeline or service needed.
3. **Make the offer specific**, so people know what they are asking for.
4. **Add context on the form's intro screen**: what happens next and how fast.
5. **Reply instantly** by text, mentioning the ad they responded to.

## How do you improve landing page conversion?

- Match the headline to the ad.
- Load fast on phones.
- Keep the form short.
- Put reviews and real photos near the form.
- Remove distracting navigation.
- Make the next step clear: what happens after they submit.

## How do you connect lead ads to your CRM?

Lead ad submissions should flow straight into your CRM through a native integration, then trigger an instant text and email and alert your team. Leaving leads to sit in Meta's lead center for a day is one of the most common reasons lead ads "do not work." ## How should you compare results?

Judge both approaches by:

- Cost per lead.
- Contact rate: percentage you actually reach.
- Appointment rate.
- Cost per customer.

Lead ads often win on cost per lead and lose on contact rate; landing pages often do the reverse. Cost per customer settles it. See [how much Facebook ads cost](/blog/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost-small-business/).

## Do special ad categories change the decision?

In housing, employment and financial categories, targeting is restricted, so offer and creative matter more. Both lead ads and landing pages work; qualifying questions and fast follow-up become even more important. See [Meta special ad categories](/blog/meta-special-ad-categories/).

## What should happen in the first five minutes after a lead comes in?

Speed decides more than format. A good setup sends an instant text naming the offer they responded to, an email with a booking link and a short introduction, and an alert to the person who will call. That person calls within minutes during business hours. If there is no answer, a second text goes out later that day. Lead ads in particular need this, because people forget forms they filled in with two taps.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Facebook lead ads worth it?

Yes, for simple offers with fast follow-up. If lead quality is poor, add qualifying questions, use the higher intent form or switch to a landing page.

### Why are my Facebook lead ad leads fake or unreachable?

Prefilled forms make it easy to submit without much thought. Higher intent forms, qualifying questions and instant replies improve contact rates.

### Do landing pages need the Meta Pixel?

Yes, plus the Conversions API ideally, so Meta can see which ads lead to form submissions and optimize for them.

### Can I use both lead ads and a landing page?

Yes. Many businesses run both and shift budget toward whichever produces customers at a lower cost.
