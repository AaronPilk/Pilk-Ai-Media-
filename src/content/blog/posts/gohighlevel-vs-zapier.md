GoHighLevel and Zapier both "automate things," which is why owners often compare them. They are not really alternatives. One is where your leads and customer conversations live; the other is the glue between apps. Here is how they differ and when you need one, the other or both.

## What does GoHighLevel do?

GoHighLevel is a CRM and marketing platform: contacts, pipelines, texting, calling, email, forms, funnels, calendars, reviews and automations in one account. Its workflow builder automates what happens inside the platform, such as instant replies to leads, pipeline moves, reminders, review requests and team alerts. See [what GoHighLevel is](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel/).

## What does Zapier do?

Zapier connects apps that do not talk to each other. A "Zap" watches for an event in one app, such as a new form entry, a paid invoice or a new spreadsheet row, and does something in another, like creating a contact, sending a message or adding a row. It connects thousands of apps and is priced by plan and the number of tasks it runs.

## When are GoHighLevel workflows enough?

If your leads, conversations, calendar and follow-up all live in GoHighLevel, its built-in workflows cover most needs:

- New lead → instant text and email → alert → task.
- Missed call → text back. See [missed call text back](/blog/missed-call-text-back/).
- Appointment booked → confirmations and reminders. See [automated appointment booking](/blog/automated-appointment-booking/).
- Job completed → review request. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/).

GoHighLevel also offers webhooks and some native integrations, which handle many connections without Zapier.

## When do you need Zapier too?

Add Zapier, or a similar tool like Make, when GoHighLevel needs to exchange data with an app it does not connect to directly:

- Sending new customers to your accounting software.
- Pulling leads from an industry platform or portal.
- Adding closed deals to a reporting spreadsheet.
- Triggering actions in project management or scheduling tools.
- Notifying a team chat channel.

## When might you use Zapier without GoHighLevel?

If you already have a CRM you like and simply need your tools connected, Zapier alone may be enough. For example, a business using HubSpot or an industry CRM can use Zapier to link forms, spreadsheets and accounting. See [GoHighLevel vs HubSpot](/blog/gohighlevel-vs-hubspot/).

## What are the downsides of chaining many automations?

- **Hidden breakage.** If one app changes, a Zap can fail quietly. Set up error alerts.
- **Task costs.** High volumes of Zaps can get expensive on task-based pricing.
- **Duplicate logic.** Automations in two places can conflict, such as two systems sending the same text.
- **No single owner.** Someone needs to know what runs where.

Keep a simple list of every automation, what triggers it and where it lives.

## How do you decide what to build where?

A practical rule: keep customer communication and follow-up in your CRM, and use Zapier only to move data in and out of it. That way, conversations stay in one inbox, and Zapier does the plumbing.

## What does a typical setup using both look like?

Take a home services company. GoHighLevel handles everything customer-facing: website form leads, instant texts, the booking calendar, reminders, the pipeline and review requests after each job. Zapier handles two jobs on the side: when a job is marked won in GoHighLevel, it creates the customer in the accounting software, and when an invoice is paid there, it tags the contact in GoHighLevel so the review request goes out. Everything the customer sees comes from one system; Zapier only moves data quietly in the background. If the accounting software later adds a native integration, the Zap can be retired without touching the customer experience.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can GoHighLevel replace Zapier?

For automations inside GoHighLevel, yes. For connecting apps GoHighLevel does not integrate with, you may still need Zapier or webhooks.

### Is Zapier hard to set up?

Simple Zaps take minutes. Multi-step automations with filters and formatting take more planning and testing.

### Does GoHighLevel have native integrations?

Yes, with some common tools, plus webhooks for custom connections. Check current integrations for the apps you use.

### Which is cheaper, GoHighLevel or Zapier?

They do different jobs, so compare total cost for your setup. Many businesses use GoHighLevel for the CRM and a small Zapier plan for a few connections.
