Hosting is one of the most confusing line items in a website budget, because the word covers everything from a bare server to a full support service. Here is what hosting actually is, what each type costs and what a small business really needs.

## What is website hosting?

Hosting is the computer, usually in a data center, that stores your website's files and sends them to visitors. When someone types your address, their browser asks your host for the page. Good hosting is fast, reliable and secure. Bad hosting is slow, goes down and gets hacked.

Hosting is separate from your **domain**, the address people type, which you register and renew yearly, and from **email**, which is often a separate service.

## What are the main types of hosting and what do they cost?

| Type | Typical cost | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Free or low-cost static hosting | Often free for small sites | Fast sites with no server-side database |
| Shared hosting | A few dollars a month | Small, low-traffic WordPress sites |
| Website builder plans (Wix, Squarespace) | Monthly plan that includes the editor | Owners who build and edit themselves |
| Managed WordPress hosting | Tens of dollars a month and up | WordPress sites that need speed and updates |
| Cloud or dedicated servers | Varies widely | Apps and high-traffic sites |
| Managed hosting with support | A monthly plan with edits included | Businesses that want it all handled |

Prices change and introductory deals often renew at a higher rate, so check the renewal price before you sign up.

## Why do some sites cost almost nothing to host?

Many modern sites are built as static files that can be served from a global network, such as Cloudflare Pages or similar services, at little or no cost. They are fast and hard to hack because there is no database or admin login on the server to attack. Forms and other dynamic features run through small separate services.

This is how we build most of our sites, which is why hosting itself is rarely the expensive part. Our own site runs this way.

## What else should you budget for besides hosting?

- **Domain name**: renewed every year. Common endings like .com are usually inexpensive; some endings cost more.
- **Business email**: often through Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, priced per user per month.
- **Form handling and CRM**: if your forms send leads to a CRM, that has its own cost.
- **Backups and monitoring**: included in good managed plans, extra on cheap ones.
- **Updates and edits**: someone has to change prices, add pages and fix things.

That last item is the real cost for most businesses. Hosting is cheap; your time or a developer's time is not.

## How do you choose the right hosting?

Match hosting to how your site is built and who maintains it:

1. **Built on Wix or Squarespace?** Hosting comes with the plan. Compare in [Wix vs custom website](/blog/wix-vs-custom-website/).
2. **WordPress, maintained by you?** Choose reputable managed WordPress hosting with backups and updates.
3. **Static site built by a developer?** Low-cost static hosting is usually ideal; budget for edits instead.
4. **Want zero hassle?** A managed plan that includes hosting, updates and edits.

## What are the warning signs of bad hosting?

- Your site is slow even on a good connection.
- It goes down and you hear about it from customers.
- Nobody knows who has the login.
- The "cheap" plan renewed at several times the original price.
- Your developer hosts the site in their own account and you cannot move it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is free website hosting good enough for a business?

For a static site on a reputable network, yes, often. Avoid free hosting that puts ads on your site or uses a subdomain instead of your own domain.

### Is hosting included in the price of a website?

Sometimes. Ask whether the build price includes hosting, for how long, and what it costs afterward. Our hosting plans are separate and optional.

### Why is my hosting bill higher than the advertised price?

Many hosts advertise a first-term discount that renews at a higher rate, and add-ons like backups or security can cost extra. Check renewal pricing before signing up.

### Do I need hosting if I use Wix or Squarespace?

No. Hosting is included in their plans. You still pay for the domain, though some plans include it for the first year.
