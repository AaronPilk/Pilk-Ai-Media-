A lead magnet is something useful you offer in exchange for contact details. The classic version is a long ebook nobody reads. The versions that work for service businesses are smaller, faster and more practical. Here are ideas by industry, what makes them convert, and how to follow up so they turn into customers.

## What makes a lead magnet convert?

- **It solves one specific problem** the right customer has right now.
- **It is quick to consume**: minutes, not hours.
- **It connects directly to your service**, so the next step is obvious.
- **The title promises a clear result**: "The 12-point roof inspection checklist" beats "Roofing guide."
- **It is easy to get**: a short form, delivered instantly.

## Which lead magnets work for local service businesses?

- **Instant estimate or price range tool**: "What will my project cost?"
- **Checklists**: "Pre-storm home checklist," "What to check before buying a used AC unit."
- **Seasonal guides**: hurricane prep, spring maintenance, holiday scheduling.
- **Free assessment or inspection**, clearly described.
- **Comparison guides**: "Tankless vs tank water heaters: which fits your home?"

## Which lead magnets work for real estate agents?

- **Home valuation**: the strongest seller offer. See [home valuation landing pages](/blog/home-valuation-landing-page/).
- **Neighborhood guides** for relocating buyers.
- **First-time buyer roadmap.**
- **Monthly market report** for a specific area.
- **Seller prep checklist**: what to fix before listing.

## Which lead magnets work for mortgage brokers?

- **Payment and affordability calculators.** See [mortgage calculators for websites](/blog/mortgage-calculator-for-website/).
- **First-time buyer guide** with assistance programs, linked to official sources.
- **Refinance review**: "Is refinancing worth it for you?"
- **Pre-approval checklist**: what documents to gather.

Keep all of them compliant with advertising rules. See [mortgage advertising compliance](/blog/mortgage-advertising-compliance/).

## Which lead magnets work for coaches and communities?

- **A free mini-course** or challenge.
- **A free community** that leads to a paid one. See [Skool free vs paid communities](/blog/skool-free-vs-paid/).
- **Templates and swipe files** members can use today.
- **A short assessment or quiz** with personalized results.

## How should a lead magnet be delivered?

1. A focused landing page describing the result. See [landing page vs website](/blog/landing-page-vs-website/).
2. A short form: name, email, phone if you will call or text.
3. Instant delivery by email, and on screen if possible.
4. An immediate text or email from a real person offering help.

Ask only for what you will use. See [lead form best practices](/blog/lead-form-best-practices/).

## What follow-up turns lead magnet downloads into customers?

Most people who download something are not ready to buy today. A good sequence:

- Day 0: deliver the resource and introduce yourself.
- Day 1: a tip on using it.
- Day 3: a short story or example of a customer with the same problem.
- Day 7: an invitation to book a call or estimate.
- Then: occasional helpful emails until they are ready.

See [automated follow-up emails](/blog/automated-follow-up-emails/) and [how to build a sales funnel](/blog/sales-funnel-for-service-business/).

## How do you know if a lead magnet is working?

Track landing page conversion rate, cost per lead if you run ads, the percentage of leads who book a call, and customers won. If people download but never book, the magnet may attract the wrong audience or sit too far from what you sell. Tighten the topic.

## What lead magnet mistakes should you avoid?

- Long ebooks with no clear next step.
- Topics too broad to attract buyers.
- Gating basic information people expect for free, like prices or service areas.
- No follow-up after the download.
- Asking for too much information up front.

## How do you pick the right lead magnet for your business?

List the three questions prospects ask most before they buy. The one that comes up most often, and that you can answer usefully in a page or a short tool, is your first lead magnet. Make sure it naturally leads to your service.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best lead magnet for a service business?

An instant estimate or a free assessment, because it connects directly to buying. Checklists and seasonal guides also work well.

### Do lead magnets still work?

Yes, when they are specific, quick and useful, and followed up promptly. Generic ebooks work much less well than they used to.

### Should I ask for a phone number on a lead magnet form?

Only if you will call or text, and with proper consent for texting. Every extra field reduces sign-ups.

### How long should a lead magnet be?

As short as possible while still solving the problem. A one-page checklist often outperforms a 40-page guide.
