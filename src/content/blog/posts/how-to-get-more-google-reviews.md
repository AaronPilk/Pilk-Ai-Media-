Reviews are one of the strongest signals for local search and the first thing many customers read before calling you. Most businesses have far fewer reviews than happy customers simply because they do not ask consistently. Here is how to fix that, and the rules that keep you out of trouble.

## Why do Google reviews matter so much?

- **Local rankings**: review count, recency and rating influence where you show up in map results.
- **Trust**: people compare reviews before choosing a business.
- **Conversion**: more recent reviews mean more calls from the same visibility.
- **AI search**: tools that summarize businesses often draw on reviews.

A steady flow of fresh reviews beats a big batch from two years ago.

## When is the best time to ask for a review?

Right after the customer feels the value:

- A contractor: when the job is finished and the customer is happy with it.
- A real estate agent: at closing or move-in.
- A mortgage broker: after funding.
- A restaurant or salon: the same day.
- An agency: after a launch or a clear win.

Ask in person if you can, then follow up with a link.

## How do you make leaving a review easy?

1. **Get your direct review link** from your Google Business Profile ("Ask for reviews" or "Get more reviews").
2. **Send it by text**, since most people review from their phone.
3. **Keep the message short and personal**:

> Hi Maria, thanks again for trusting us with your kitchen. If you have a minute, a quick Google review would mean a lot to our small team: [link]

4. **Send one reminder** a few days later if they have not reviewed.

## How do you automate review requests?

Connect review requests to the moment a job is marked complete in your CRM:

- Job completed → thank-you text → review request with link a day later → one reminder.
- Track who reviewed so you stop asking them.
- Route complaints to a person quickly so you can fix problems.

## What are the rules for asking for reviews?

This is general information, not legal advice:

- **No incentives.** Google's policies prohibit offering money, discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews.
- **No review gating.** Do not screen customers and only send happy ones to Google. Ask everyone.
- **No fake reviews.** Do not write your own, have employees pose as customers or buy reviews.
- **FTC rules.** The Federal Trade Commission's rule on consumer reviews, in effect since 2024, prohibits fake reviews, buying reviews and suppressing negative ones, with penalties for violations.
- **Do not review-bomb competitors** or ask others to.

Breaking these rules can get reviews removed, your profile restricted or worse.

## How should you reply to reviews?

- **Reply to every review**, good and bad, within a few days.
- **Positive reviews**: thank them by name and mention the service naturally.
- **Negative reviews**: stay calm, acknowledge the issue, avoid sharing private details, and offer to resolve it offline. Future customers read how you respond.
- **Fake or abusive reviews**: report them through your Business Profile.

## How many reviews do you need?

There is no magic number. Aim to have more recent, genuine reviews than the businesses you compete with in map results, and keep them coming every month. Consistency signals an active business.

## What should a review request say?

Keep it personal, short and specific. Use the customer's name, mention the job, thank them, and make the link the only thing to click. Avoid asking for a "five-star review"; ask for honest feedback. Send it while the experience is fresh, and from a person rather than a generic company name. Customers respond to people, not campaigns.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's policies prohibit incentives for reviews, and FTC rules also restrict buying reviews.

### Is it okay to ask only happy customers for reviews?

No. Selectively soliciting positive reviews, often called review gating, is against Google's policies. Ask every customer.

### How do I get my Google review link?

Open your Google Business Profile, find the option to ask for or get more reviews, and copy the short link it gives you.

### Can I remove a bad Google review?

Only if it violates Google's policies, such as spam, fake content or harassment. Otherwise, respond professionally and focus on earning more good reviews.
