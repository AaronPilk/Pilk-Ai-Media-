If you have been near a marketing agency lately, you have probably heard "we'll set you up in GHL." GoHighLevel, often shortened to GHL or HighLevel, has become the default platform for agencies that run lead generation for local businesses. We build most of our client CRMs in it. Here is what it actually is, what it is good at, what it costs and where it falls short.

## What does GoHighLevel do?

GoHighLevel puts tools that small businesses usually buy separately into one account:

- **CRM**: every contact, with their history, notes, tags and conversations.
- **Pipelines**: stages like New Lead, Contacted, Booked and Won, so you can see every deal at a glance.
- **Conversations inbox**: texts, emails, Facebook and Instagram messages, Google Business Profile chat and website chat in one place.
- **Forms, surveys and funnels**: landing pages and forms that feed straight into the CRM.
- **Calendars**: booking links with reminders.
- **Workflows**: automations that send texts and emails, move deals, assign tasks and alert your team.
- **Reputation management**: automated review requests and review monitoring.
- **Phone system**: business numbers, call tracking and missed call text back.
- **Courses and communities**: membership areas for coaches and course creators.

The appeal is that these pieces talk to each other without stitching five tools together.

## Who is GoHighLevel built for?

GoHighLevel was designed for agencies. An agency holds the main account and creates a "sub-account" for each client business. That is why many owners first see GHL with their agency's branding on it, sometimes under a different name.

It suits:

- **Local service businesses** that live on calls, forms and bookings.
- **Real estate agents and mortgage brokers** who need fast follow-up and long nurture sequences. See [GoHighLevel for real estate](/blog/gohighlevel-for-real-estate/).
- **Coaches and course creators** who want funnels, payments and memberships in one place.
- **Agencies** managing many clients.

It is less suited to large sales teams with complex reporting needs, or businesses that only need a simple contact list.

## How much does GoHighLevel cost?

HighLevel's published plans are Starter at $97 a month, Unlimited at $297 a month and Agency Pro at $497 a month, with yearly billing at ten times the monthly price. Usage is extra: text messages, emails, phone minutes and AI features are billed based on how much you use them. Check HighLevel's own pricing page for the current figures before you budget.

## What can GoHighLevel automate for a small business?

The automations that tend to pay off first:

1. **Instant text and email reply** to every new form lead.
2. **Missed call text back**, so callers who reach voicemail get a reply in seconds. See [missed call text back](/blog/missed-call-text-back/).
3. **Appointment reminders** that cut no-shows.
4. **Follow-up sequences** for leads who do not book right away.
5. **Review requests** after a job is finished.
6. **Pipeline moves and team alerts**, so nobody has to remember to update the CRM.

Speed matters most. Our guide to [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/) explains why the first reply wins, and our [AI automation and chatbots](/services/ai-automation-chatbots/) page shows how we set these up.

## What are the downsides of GoHighLevel?

It is powerful, but it is not perfect:

- **It has a learning curve.** There are a lot of settings, and a messy setup is worse than a simple one.
- **Usage fees add up** if you send a lot of texts or emails.
- **Texting requires carrier registration** in the US, known as A2P 10DLC, which can delay launch. We cover it in [A2P 10DLC registration](/blog/a2p-10dlc-registration/).
- **Some built-in tools are good enough rather than best in class**, for example the website and blog builder compared with a dedicated site.
- **Account ownership** can be murky when the sub-account sits under an agency. Ask how you would move your data if you leave.

## How do you know if your business needs GoHighLevel?

You are a good fit if leads come from several places, follow-up depends on someone remembering, and you lose customers because replies are slow. You probably do not need it if you get a few inquiries a month and answer them all personally the same day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is GoHighLevel a CRM?

Yes, and more. The CRM is the core, with pipelines, forms, funnels, calendars, messaging, automations and review tools built around it.

### Can a single business use GoHighLevel without an agency?

Yes. Anyone can sign up for a plan. Many single businesses still prefer an agency sub-account because setup is the hard part and the agency handles it.

### Does GoHighLevel include texting?

Yes, through its built-in phone system or a connected provider, with per-message usage fees. US businesses must complete A2P 10DLC registration before sending most texts.

### Who owns my data in GoHighLevel?

Your contacts and leads belong to you, but if your sub-account sits under an agency's account, moving it depends on the platform's transfer options and your agreement. Ask before you sign up.
