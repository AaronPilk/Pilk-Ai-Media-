More people now ask ChatGPT for recommendations the way they used to ask Google: "best web designer in St. Petersburg," "how much does a kitchen remodel cost in Tampa." When ChatGPT searches the web to answer, it cites sources and sometimes names businesses. There is no paid placement and no secret trick, but there are clear steps that make your business easier to find, understand and cite.

## How does ChatGPT search find websites?

When ChatGPT decides a question needs current information, it searches the web and summarises what it finds, with links to its sources. OpenAI runs its own crawler for this, called OAI-SearchBot, and OpenAI says it also works with third-party search providers. In practice, that means two things matter:

1. **OpenAI's crawler must be allowed** to read your site.
2. **Your site should be well indexed in regular search engines**, especially Bing, since search results feed what AI tools see.

OpenAI documents its crawlers on its [bots page](https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots). The short version: OAI-SearchBot is for search, GPTBot is for training its models, and ChatGPT-User fetches a page when a person asks ChatGPT to look at it.

## Is your website blocking ChatGPT?

Check your robots.txt file, at yourdomain.com/robots.txt. If it blocks OAI-SearchBot, or blocks all bots, ChatGPT search cannot read your pages. Some website platforms, security plugins and CDN bot settings block AI crawlers by default, so check those too.

You can block GPTBot (training) and still allow OAI-SearchBot (search). Many businesses do exactly that. If you want to be recommended, do not block the search crawler.

## What kind of content does ChatGPT cite?

AI search tools tend to cite pages that answer the question directly and clearly. That means:

- **A short, direct answer near the top** of the page, followed by detail.
- **Headings written as the questions people ask**, like "How much does a website cost?"
- **Specific facts**: prices or price ranges, timelines, locations served, what is and is not included.
- **Named authors and dates**, so the page looks current and accountable.
- **FAQ sections** that cover the follow-up questions.

## Do listings and reviews help you show up in ChatGPT?

Yes, indirectly. When ChatGPT searches for local businesses, it often draws on directories, review sites, maps listings and articles, not just your own site. What helps:

1. **Consistent name, address, phone and website** across Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp and industry directories.
2. **Real reviews** that mention the services you provide and the places you serve.
3. **Mentions on other trusted sites**: local news, associations, partners and client sites.
4. **A clear "about" page** that says who you are, where you are and what you do.

## Does schema markup help with ChatGPT?

Structured data, such as Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage and Article markup, helps search engines understand your pages, and anything that helps search engines understand you helps the tools that rely on them. It is not a magic switch, and it does not replace clear writing on the page.

## Do you need an llms.txt file?

An llms.txt file is a proposed plain-text summary of your site for AI tools. It is cheap to add and harmless, and we publish one on our own site. But no major AI search tool has said it relies on it, and Google has said you do not need one for its search features. Treat it as optional, not as a ranking factor. We explain the format in [what llms.txt is](/blog/what-is-llms-txt/).

## How do you check if ChatGPT mentions your business?

Ask it. Try the questions your customers would ask, with and without your city. Note which businesses and sources it cites. Then:

- Check whether your pages appear among the sources.
- Look at which sites it cites for competitors; those are places to get listed or mentioned.
- Repeat every month or two, since answers change.

Watch your analytics for referral traffic from chatgpt.com as well; it is small for most businesses today but growing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT search?

No. As of late 2026 there is no paid placement in ChatGPT's search answers. Visibility comes from being crawlable, indexed, clearly written and mentioned on trusted sites.

### Should I block GPTBot?

That is your choice. Blocking GPTBot stops OpenAI from using your content to train models. It does not remove you from ChatGPT search, which uses OAI-SearchBot. Make sure you are not blocking the search crawler by mistake.

### How long does it take to show up in ChatGPT?

There is no fixed timeline. Once your site is crawlable and indexed, new pages can be found within days or weeks. Building the reviews and mentions that earn recommendations takes months.

### Does Bing matter for ChatGPT search?

Being well indexed in Bing helps, since search results from third-party providers feed AI tools. Submit your sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools and claim Bing Places for local businesses.
