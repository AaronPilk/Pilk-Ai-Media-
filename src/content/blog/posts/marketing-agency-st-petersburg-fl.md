## What services do St. Petersburg marketing agencies offer?

Most agencies combine several of these:

- **Websites**: design, development, hosting and updates.
- **Local SEO**: Google Business Profile, listings, reviews and service pages.
- **Paid ads**: Google search ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads.
- **Social media**: content creation, posting and community management.
- **Lead funnels and CRM**: landing pages, follow-up automation, pipelines.
- **AI and automation**: chatbots, missed call text back, workflows.
- **Branding and content**: logos, photography, video and copy.

Few agencies are excellent at all of them. Ask what they specialize in and what they outsource.

## What should a local business look for in an agency?

1. **Clear pricing.** You should know roughly what things cost before a sales call, or get a written quote right after.
2. **Real work you can visit.** Live sites, real campaigns and named clients where they have permission.
3. **Ownership.** Your domain, website, ad accounts, Google profile and CRM should be in your name.
4. **Focus on leads, not vanity metrics.** Likes and impressions are not customers.
5. **Direct access** to the people doing the work.
6. **Honest advice**, including telling you when something is not worth the money.

## How much does a marketing agency in Tampa Bay cost?

Pricing varies widely by agency size and scope. Common structures:

- **Project fees** for websites and setup work.
- **Monthly retainers** for ongoing SEO, social or ads management.
- **Ad spend** paid to Google or Meta, separate from management fees.
- **Hourly consulting** for advice and audits.

## What questions should you ask a marketing agency?

- Who will actually do the work?
- What is included, and what costs extra?
- How long until I should expect results, and how will we measure them?
- Who owns the accounts and assets?
- What happens if we stop working together?
- Can I speak to a current client?

For website-specific questions, see [questions to ask a web designer](/blog/questions-to-ask-a-web-designer/).

## Does an agency need to be local?

Not always. Websites, ads and automations work fine remotely. A local agency helps when you want in-person meetings, on-site photography or video, or someone who knows the area's neighborhoods and seasonal patterns. Tampa Bay's mix of tourism, real estate and service businesses has its own rhythms, from hurricane season to snowbird months.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I choose a marketing agency in St. Petersburg?

Compare specialties, pricing transparency, live examples, account ownership terms and whether you will work directly with the people doing the work.

### Is it better to hire a local or remote marketing agency?

Remote agencies can do excellent work. Local helps for in-person meetings, on-site content and local market knowledge.

### What should a small business spend on marketing?

It depends on your margins and goals. Start with a solid website, local search and follow-up, then add ad spend you can sustain while measuring cost per customer.

### Do marketing agencies require long contracts?

Some do. Many smaller agencies work month to month for ongoing services. Read the cancellation terms before signing.
