"Should we advertise on Instagram or Facebook?" sounds like a big decision, but in practice it rarely is. Both are part of Meta, both are managed in the same Ads Manager, and the same campaign can run on both. The real decisions are about creative, offer and audience. Here is how the two placements differ and how to use them together.

## How are Instagram and Facebook ads managed?

Both run through Meta Ads Manager. When you create a campaign, you choose placements: Facebook feed, Instagram feed, Stories, Reels, Messenger, the Audience Network and more. Advantage+ placements, Meta's automatic option, lets Meta distribute your budget across them based on performance.

## How do the audiences differ?

| | Facebook | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Broad, strong among adults 30+ | Skews younger, highly visual |
| Mindset | Community, groups, local news, marketplace | Discovery, inspiration, entertainment |
| Strong for | Local services, events, lead forms | Lifestyle, real estate visuals, beauty, food, fashion |
| Top formats | Feed, video, lead forms | Reels, Stories, feed |

These are tendencies, not rules. Plenty of older buyers use Instagram, and plenty of younger ones use Facebook Marketplace and groups.

## Which formats work best on each?

- **Instagram Reels and Stories**: vertical video, quick hooks, captions, native-looking content.
- **Facebook feed**: video, carousels, single images with clear offers, and lead forms.
- **Both**: short vertical video tends to perform well across placements.

Create vertical (9:16) versions of your video so it fits Reels and Stories, and square or 4:5 versions for feeds.

## Is Instagram more expensive than Facebook?

Costs depend on audience, competition, placement and creative quality more than the app. Some placements are cheaper per impression; others convert better. Letting Meta optimize across placements usually finds the best cost per result. Judge by cost per lead and customer, not cost per click. See [how much Facebook ads cost](/blog/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost-small-business/).

## When should you limit ads to one platform?

- Your creative only works in one format, such as polished vertical video for Reels.
- Results data clearly shows one placement converts and the other wastes spend.
- Your audience is concentrated on one app, based on your own data.

Even then, test again later, as performance changes over time.

## What creative works across both?

- Real people and real work, not stock imagery.
- A hook in the first two seconds.
- Captions on every video.
- One clear message and offer.
- Several variations so Meta can find winners.

## How should you track results across both?

Use the Meta Pixel and Conversions API so conversions count regardless of placement, then break down results by placement in Ads Manager. See [Meta Pixel vs Conversions API](/blog/meta-pixel-vs-conversions-api/). If campaigns underperform, see [why Facebook ads are not working](/blog/why-are-my-facebook-ads-not-working/).

## What should a small business do first?

Start with one campaign, one offer and automatic placements across both apps, with vertical and square video versions. After a few weeks, review placement results and adjust creative for whichever placements perform best.

## How should a local business split its creative between the two?

You do not need separate campaigns, but you do need the right shapes. Film each video vertically so it works in Reels and Stories, then export a square or 4:5 crop for feeds. Write a caption that works without the video's sound and keep the first line short, since both apps cut captions off. If you have photos, a carousel of before-and-after shots tends to work well in feeds on both apps. After a few weeks, check placement breakdowns: if Reels drives cheap leads, make more vertical video; if Facebook feed leads close better, give it more attention in your creative.

## What results should you expect in the first month?

Expect the first two weeks to be a learning period where costs move around as Meta tests placements and audiences. By the end of the month you should know which offer and creative produce leads at a cost you can live with, and which placements deliver them. Judge by leads and booked appointments, not likes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I run Instagram ads without a Facebook page?

Meta ads are managed through Meta's business tools, which typically connect a Facebook page and Instagram account. Set up both for the most options.

### Should I boost Instagram posts or use Ads Manager?

Ads Manager offers proper objectives, tracking and targeting. Boosting is fine for awareness but rarely best for leads.

### Do Instagram ads work for local businesses?

Yes, especially with local video and clear offers. Many local businesses run Instagram and Facebook together in one campaign.

### What size should Instagram ad videos be?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories, and square or 4:5 for feeds. Making both versions covers most placements.
