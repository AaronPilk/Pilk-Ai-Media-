Title is a relationship business with strict rules about how relationships are built. You cannot pay for referrals, and in states like Florida where title insurance premiums are set by the state, you cannot compete on premium price either. You compete on service, speed and trust. These marketing ideas focus on exactly that.

## What makes title company marketing different?

Two things shape everything:

1. **RESPA Section 8** prohibits giving anything of value in exchange for referrals of settlement business. Gifts, free services or paid marketing tied to referrals can create real legal risk. Normal promotional and educational activities are allowed when they are not tied to referrals and do not pay the referrer's own expenses.
2. **Your customers are often referral partners**, realtors, lenders, attorneys and builders, who choose title for their clients. They value reliability above all.

This is general information, not legal advice. Run any program involving partners past your underwriter or attorney.

## What website features help a title company win business?

Your website should save agents and lenders time:

- **Online order opening**, so an agent can open a file in two minutes from a phone.
- **A closing cost or premium calculator** so agents can answer clients quickly. See [title insurance calculators](/blog/title-insurance-calculator/).
- **A wire fraud warning page** linked from every page and closing email. See [real estate wire fraud](/blog/real-estate-wire-fraud/).
- **Closing guides** for buyers and sellers, so clients arrive prepared.
- **Team pages** with names, photos and direct contacts.
- **Office locations** with hours and directions.

## How can a title company market to real estate agents?

- **Make your closings the best part of the transaction.** Agents talk. A calm, on-time closing is your best marketing.
- **Offer education**: short sessions on topics like title issues that delay closings, probate sales or wire fraud. Keep them educational, not tied to referrals.
- **Send useful updates**: a monthly email on local market trends, title tips and changes that affect closings.
- **Be reachable.** Publish direct numbers and answer them. Respond to order questions within minutes.
- **Help new agents** understand the closing process; they remember who helped them early.

## Should title companies use social media?

Yes, as a trust builder rather than a lead machine. Share closing celebrations (with permission), team introductions, quick title tips, local events and fraud warnings. Short videos explaining common closing questions work well. Our post on [title company social media](/blog/title-company-social-media/) has a content plan.

## How do reviews help title companies?

Buyers and sellers search your name before closing, especially when an agent recommends you. Ask every client for a Google review after closing, with a direct link, and respond to every review. A steady stream of recent reviews also helps your map rankings. Automating the request is simple; see [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/).

## Can title companies use search to get business?

Consumers rarely search for title companies, but they do search for answers: "what is owner's title insurance," "how long does closing take," "do I need title insurance on a cash purchase." Good answers on your site build trust with buyers and give agents something to share. Pair that with a complete Google Business Profile and consistent listings. Our [local SEO checklist](/blog/local-seo-checklist-for-small-businesses/) covers the basics.

## What about joint ventures and affiliated businesses?

Many title agencies operate as joint ventures with brokerages, builders or lenders. These are legal when structured properly and disclosed, but they come with specific rules on disclosure and on how partners are paid. See [affiliated business arrangement disclosures](/blog/affiliated-business-arrangement-disclosure/) and talk to your attorney before launching one.

## What title company marketing should you avoid?

- Gifts, free services or paid co-marketing tied to referrals.
- Paying for an agent's marketing, events or expenses.
- Promising a closing date you cannot control.
- Generic stock-photo websites with no way to order online.
- Collecting sensitive information through unsecured forms.

## Where should a title company start?

1. A fast website with online ordering and a wire fraud page.
2. A review request after every closing.
3. A simple monthly email to your agent and lender list.
4. One educational session per quarter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a title company give gifts to realtors?

Gifts or anything of value given in exchange for referrals can violate RESPA Section 8. Check with your attorney or underwriter before any gift or partner program.

### Can title companies compete on price in Florida?

Florida title insurance premiums are set by the state, so agencies mainly compete on service, speed and experience. Some other closing fees vary by company.

### Is social media worth it for a title company?

As a trust builder, yes. Team posts, closing tips and fraud warnings keep you visible to agents and reassure buyers who look you up.

### What is the best marketing for a new title agency?

A strong website with online ordering, flawless early closings, steady reviews and regular, genuinely useful education for local agents and lenders.
