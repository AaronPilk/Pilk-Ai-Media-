When a potential customer calls and you do not pick up, most of them will not leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list. Missed call text back catches them in the seconds after the missed call. Here is how it works and how to set it up well.

## How does missed call text back work?

1. A customer calls your business number.
2. Nobody answers, because you are on a job, with a client or closed.
3. Within seconds, your phone system sends them a text from the same business number.
4. They reply by text, and the conversation lands in your inbox or CRM.
5. You or your team pick it up as soon as possible, or automation books the appointment.

It requires a business phone number that supports texting, usually through your CRM or business phone provider.

## Why does missed call text back work so well?

- **It is instant.** The caller still has their phone in hand.
- **Texting is easier** than waiting on hold or leaving a voicemail.
- **It shows you are responsive**, even when you are busy.
- **It starts a written record** with the caller's needs.

It is a direct application of [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/): the first business to respond usually gets the conversation.

## What should the missed call text say?

Keep it short, human and specific:

> Hi, this is Sam from Bayside Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call. We're on a job right now. What can we help you with? You can also book a time here: [link]

Good texts:

- Name the business and a person.
- Apologize briefly and say when you will call back.
- Ask an open question.
- Offer a booking link.
- Avoid sounding like a robot or a sales pitch.

## What are the rules for missed call texts?

This is general information, not legal advice. A text replying to someone who just called you is generally treated as responding to their inquiry, but the rules around automated and marketing texts are strict:

- **Register for A2P 10DLC.** US carriers require business texting from standard local numbers to be registered, or messages get blocked. See [A2P 10DLC registration](/blog/a2p-10dlc-registration/).
- **Keep it informational.** The first text should respond to the call, not push promotions.
- **Get consent before marketing texts.** Ongoing promotional texts need clear permission. See [TCPA text message consent](/blog/tcpa-text-message-consent/).
- **Honor opt-outs** immediately when someone replies STOP.
- **Do not text landlines or numbers that cannot receive texts**; good systems skip them.

## How do you set up missed call text back?

Most CRM platforms with a phone system include it. In GoHighLevel, for example, it is a standard workflow: a missed call triggers a text from the business number. See [what GoHighLevel is](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel/).

Setup steps:

1. Get or port a business number into a system that supports calls and texts.
2. Complete carrier registration for texting.
3. Write the missed call message.
4. Set business hours and an after-hours version of the message.
5. Route replies to the right person with an instant notification.
6. Test from several phones.

## What should happen after the text?

The text only starts the conversation. Have a plan:

- Notify the right person immediately.
- Reply to responses within minutes during business hours.
- If the caller does not reply, try a call back later that day.
- Log the lead in your CRM with the source "missed call."
- Track how many missed calls turn into booked jobs.

## Is missed call text back better than an AI receptionist?

They solve the same problem differently. Missed call text back is cheaper, simpler and less likely to annoy anyone. An AI receptionist can handle the whole call but costs more and needs careful setup. Many businesses start with text back and add a voice agent only if call volume justifies it. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does missed call text back work with my existing number?

Often yes, by porting your number to a phone system that supports texting, or by forwarding calls. Check with your provider before porting.

### Is missed call text back legal?

A text responding to someone who just called is generally treated as a response to their inquiry. Register for business texting, keep the first message informational and honor opt-outs.

### How fast should the text go out?

Within seconds of the missed call. The value comes from reaching the caller before they call someone else.

### What if the caller was a spam call?

Good systems filter known spam and only text numbers that can receive texts. Some spam replies are inevitable and easy to ignore.
