GoHighLevel and HubSpot both call themselves CRMs, but they were built for different businesses. Picking the wrong one means paying for features you never use or fighting a tool that does not fit how you sell. This comparison is written by a studio that builds in GoHighLevel most days, so we will be upfront about where HubSpot wins.

## What is the main difference between GoHighLevel and HubSpot?

**GoHighLevel** was designed for marketing agencies serving local businesses. It bundles the tools a service business needs to capture and convert leads: CRM, pipelines, texting, calls, funnels, forms, calendars, review requests and automations. Pricing is a flat monthly plan plus usage. See [what GoHighLevel is](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel/).

**HubSpot** grew from inbound marketing into a full customer platform with separate hubs for marketing, sales, service, content and operations. It has a free CRM to start, and paid tiers priced by hub, tier and seat.

## When does GoHighLevel make more sense?

- Your leads come from calls, forms, texts and local ads.
- Speed of follow-up matters more than complex reporting.
- You want texting, missed call text back and review requests without extra tools. See [missed call text back](/blog/missed-call-text-back/).
- You want funnels and landing pages in the same place as your CRM. See [landing page vs website](/blog/landing-page-vs-website/).
- You prefer a predictable flat monthly cost.

## When does HubSpot make more sense?

- You run a sales team with several reps, longer sales cycles and deal stages to manage.
- You need deep reporting, forecasting and attribution.
- You rely on many integrations with other business software.
- You want a polished interface that new staff learn quickly.
- You are starting very small and the free CRM covers your needs.

## How do the costs compare over time?

GoHighLevel's cost stays fairly flat as you add users, with usage fees for texts, emails and calls. HubSpot's cost grows with seats and tiers, and higher tiers can carry onboarding fees. For a five-person local service business that needs texting and funnels, GoHighLevel is often cheaper. For a B2B team that needs advanced sales tools, HubSpot's cost can be justified by what it does.

## Which is easier to set up?

HubSpot is easier to learn on day one. GoHighLevel has more settings and a steeper learning curve, which is why most single businesses get it set up by an agency. A messy GoHighLevel setup is worse than a simple one, so plan pipelines, forms and automations before building.

## Can you switch from HubSpot to GoHighLevel or back?

Yes. Contacts, companies and deals export as spreadsheets, and both platforms import them. Automations, templates and reports have to be rebuilt. Plan the move during a quiet period and run both briefly to avoid missing leads.

## Do you need a CRM at all?

If you get only a few inquiries a month and answer them yourself the same day, maybe not yet. If leads come from several places or follow-up depends on memory, yes. See [do I need a CRM](/blog/do-i-need-a-crm/) and [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/).

## What should you ask before choosing either CRM?

Write down how leads reach you today, who follows up, how fast, and what reports you actually look at. Then ask each option: Can it text and call from one inbox? Can it send instant replies and reminders? How are users and usage priced at our size? Who will set it up and maintain it? Can we export our data if we leave? The platform that answers those questions for your process is the right one, whatever its feature list says.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is GoHighLevel cheaper than HubSpot?

For local service businesses that need texting, funnels and review requests, often yes. For very small teams, HubSpot's free CRM costs nothing to start.

### Can HubSpot send text messages?

HubSpot's texting options depend on plan and integrations. Many businesses connect a texting app. GoHighLevel includes texting natively, with usage fees.

### Which CRM is better for real estate agents?

Many agents prefer GoHighLevel for texting, nurture sequences and lead routing. Teams with complex reporting needs sometimes choose HubSpot or a real estate-specific CRM.

### Can I use both GoHighLevel and HubSpot?

You can, but running two CRMs usually creates duplicate data and confusion. Pick one as the source of truth.
