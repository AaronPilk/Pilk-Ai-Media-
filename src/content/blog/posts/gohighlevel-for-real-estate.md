Real estate leads are slow to mature and quick to forget you. An agent might get a lead today who buys in nine months, and the agent who stayed in touch wins. GoHighLevel is popular with agents and teams because it makes that kind of follow-up practical. Here is how to set it up for real estate and which workflows matter most.

## Why do real estate agents use GoHighLevel?

- **All leads in one inbox**: website forms, Facebook lead ads, portal leads, calls, texts and social messages.
- **Built-in texting and calling**, which is how most real estate conversations happen.
- **Automations** for instant replies, reminders and long-term nurture.
- **Landing pages and funnels** for valuations, listings and open houses.
- **Pipelines** for buyers, sellers and referrals.
- **Review requests** after closing.

See [what GoHighLevel is](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel/) for the platform overview.

## What pipelines should a real estate account have?

Keep them simple:

- **Buyers**: New lead, Contacted, Appointment set, Actively looking, Under contract, Closed.
- **Sellers**: New lead, Valuation sent, Listing appointment, Listed, Under contract, Closed.
- **Long-term nurture**: for leads more than six months out.
- **Referral partners**: lenders, past clients and other agents who send business.

## Which workflows matter most for real estate?

**1. Instant lead response.** New lead from any source → text within a minute, email with your intro, alert to the agent, task to call. See [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/).

**2. Missed call text back.** Any missed call gets a text from your business number. See [missed call text back](/blog/missed-call-text-back/).

**3. Home valuation follow-up.** Valuation request → confirmation text → personal valuation within a day → follow-up sequence → listing appointment offer. See [home valuation landing pages](/blog/home-valuation-landing-page/).

**4. Long-term nurture.** Monthly market updates, new listings matching their area, and personal check-ins every few months for leads not ready yet.

**5. Showing and appointment reminders.** Automatic confirmations and reminders cut no-shows.

**6. Post-closing.** Thank-you, review request, home anniversary messages and referral asks.

## How do you connect lead sources to GoHighLevel?

- **Your website**: forms post directly into the CRM with source and lead type.
- **Facebook and Instagram lead ads**: native integration.
- **Portals**: lead emails or integrations route into the CRM.
- **Open houses**: a sign-in form on a tablet or QR code.
- **Calls and texts**: your business number lives in the CRM.

Tag every lead by source so you know which channels produce closings.

## What rules apply to real estate texting and ads?

- **Texting**: register for A2P 10DLC and get proper consent for marketing texts. See [A2P 10DLC registration](/blog/a2p-10dlc-registration/).
- **Ads**: housing ads on Meta run under special ad category limits. See [Facebook ads for real estate agents](/blog/facebook-ads-for-real-estate-agents/).
- **Fair housing**: all automated messages and landing pages follow the same rules as any advertising.

## What mistakes do agents make with GoHighLevel?

- Building dozens of automations before the basics work.
- Messages that sound robotic or send too often.
- No one assigned to reply when leads respond.
- Pipelines nobody updates.
- Skipping texting registration, so messages never arrive.

Start with the six workflows above, test them on your own phone, then add more.

## How long does a real estate GoHighLevel setup take?

A focused setup with lead capture, instant response, missed call text back, two pipelines and a review request can be live within a couple of weeks, once texting registration is approved. Long-term nurture content, listing funnels and team routing come next. The order matters: get every lead captured and answered fast before building anything clever. Plan an hour each week for the first month to review conversations, fix messages that sound wrong and adjust timing. Agents who skip that review end up with automations that run but do not convert.
