Mortgage SEO is harder than most local SEO. You compete with national lenders and big comparison sites, and Google holds financial content to a higher standard because bad advice can hurt people. The good news: local brokers can still win searches in their own market. Here is how.

## Why is SEO harder for mortgage brokers?

Google treats money topics as "your money or your life" content, where accuracy and trust matter more. It looks for signs that real, qualified people stand behind the content. National lenders have huge sites and budgets. And mortgage pages have to stay compliant, which rules out a lot of the punchy copy that works elsewhere.

You win by being specific: your market, your programs, your people.

## What pages does a mortgage broker website need for SEO?

1. **One page per loan program you offer**: conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, refinance, cash-out, and niche programs like self-employed or investor loans. Each should explain who it fits, typical requirements in plain terms, and how you help.
2. **Local pages** for the cities and counties you serve, with real local information, not a template with the city name swapped.
3. **About and team pages** with names, photos, experience and NMLS numbers.
4. **First-time buyer resources**, including state and local assistance programs, linked to the official program pages since details change.
5. **Calculators** for payment, affordability and refinance. See [mortgage calculators for websites](/blog/mortgage-calculator-for-website/).
6. **FAQ sections** answering the questions borrowers actually ask.

## How do mortgage brokers rank in local search?

For "mortgage broker near me" searches, the map results matter most. That means:

- **A complete Google Business Profile** with the right primary category, accurate hours, services and photos. Follow Google's rules on addresses and service areas; see [Google Business Profile for service area businesses](/blog/google-business-profile-service-area-business/).
- **Reviews**, steadily, from real borrowers, mentioning the loan type and area when they naturally do.
- **Consistent listings** on Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and financial directories. See [Bing Places for Business](/blog/bing-places-for-business/).
- **Local links** from realtors, builders, associations and community sponsorships.

## What content ranks for mortgage brokers?

Content that answers real questions better than anyone else in your market:

- "How much do I need for a down payment in [your county]?"
- "FHA vs conventional for first-time buyers"
- "Can I get a mortgage if I am self-employed?"
- "What does a mortgage broker do differently from a bank?"
- Local market updates written by your team.

Put a direct answer at the top, use the question as a heading, and name the author. Keep it compliant: avoid trigger terms without disclosures and never promise approval. See [mortgage advertising compliance](/blog/mortgage-advertising-compliance/).

## Which trust signals matter for mortgage SEO?

- NMLS numbers for the company and loan officers, with a link to NMLS Consumer Access.
- Named authors with real credentials on every article.
- Dates showing when content was last checked.
- A physical presence where you have one, and consistent contact details.
- Reviews on Google and other platforms.
- A secure site, fast pages and a clear privacy policy.

## How long does mortgage SEO take?

Expect several months before meaningful results for competitive terms, faster for very specific local and long-tail questions. A new site can start showing up for niche questions within weeks once indexed. Our guide to [how long SEO takes](/blog/how-long-does-seo-take/) explains what to expect at each stage.

## What mortgage SEO mistakes should you avoid?

- Copying program descriptions from lenders or other brokers.
- City pages that are identical except for the city name.
- Static rates that go stale and create compliance risk.
- Buying links or using link schemes.
- Ignoring your Google Business Profile.
- Burying NMLS details in tiny footer text only.

## Can a mortgage website rank in AI search too?

Yes, and the same work helps. Clear answers, named experts and consistent business information make it easier for Google's AI Overviews and tools like ChatGPT to cite you. See [how to show up in Google AI Overviews](/blog/how-to-show-up-in-google-ai-overviews/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a small mortgage broker outrank big lenders?

For local and specific searches, yes. National lenders rarely have strong pages about your county, your local programs or your team. That is where you win.

### Should mortgage brokers blog?

Helpful, accurate articles that answer borrower questions work well. Quality and accuracy matter more than frequency.

### Do mortgage brokers need a Google Business Profile?

Yes, if you serve borrowers in a local area. It drives map results and reviews. Follow Google's rules on addresses and service areas.

### Is mortgage SEO different from regular SEO?

The fundamentals are the same. The differences are higher trust standards for financial content and advertising rules that limit what you can say.
