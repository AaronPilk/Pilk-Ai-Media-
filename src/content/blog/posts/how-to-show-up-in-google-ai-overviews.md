AI Overviews, the AI-written summaries at the top of many Google results, and AI Mode, Google's conversational search, change how people see search results. Owners keep asking how to "get into" them. Google's own answer is refreshingly direct, and it is worth reading before you pay anyone for "AI SEO."

## What does Google say about ranking in AI Overviews?

Google's documentation on [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and no special optimisation needed. To be eligible as a supporting link, a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown in Google Search with a snippet.

Google's guidance on generative AI and search, updated in May 2026, says the same thing in more detail: focus on helpful, people-first content and standard SEO best practices. It also says you do not need special files such as llms.txt for Google's AI features.

## What actually helps a page get cited in AI Overviews?

Because AI Overviews draw on pages Google already trusts for a query, the practical steps are the fundamentals done well:

1. **Get indexed.** Submit your sitemap in Google Search Console and fix indexing errors.
2. **Answer the question early.** Put a clear, direct answer near the top, then explain.
3. **Use question headings.** Match the way people phrase searches.
4. **Be specific.** Prices, timelines, locations and steps are easier to cite than vague claims.
5. **Show who wrote it and when.** A named author, an about page and visible dates support trust.
6. **Keep the page fast and accessible.** Clean HTML, readable text and good mobile performance.
7. **Cover follow-up questions** in an FAQ section.

We cover the step most sites skip in [Google Search Console for beginners](/blog/google-search-console-for-beginners/).

## Does schema markup help with AI Overviews?

Google uses structured data to understand pages, and it remains useful for rich results. Google says structured data is not required for AI features. Add accurate schema where it fits, such as Organization, LocalBusiness, Article and FAQPage, and make sure it matches what is on the page.

## Can you stop Google using your content in AI Overviews?

Yes, with tradeoffs. Google's snippet controls, nosnippet and max-snippet, limit what appears in AI Overviews as well as regular snippets. Blocking Google-Extended controls whether your content is used for Gemini models, but Google says it does not affect your appearance in Search or AI Overviews. Most small businesses want to be cited, so they leave these alone.

## Do AI Overviews reduce clicks to your website?

For some informational searches, yes: if the summary answers the question, fewer people click. For local and commercial searches, people still compare businesses, read reviews and visit sites before calling. Two things protect you:

- **Be the cited source**, so your brand appears in the answer.
- **Make your site worth the click**: real prices, examples of work, clear next steps.

Track this in Search Console. AI Overview impressions and clicks are counted in your normal Performance report, so watch query-level trends rather than expecting a separate AI report.

## How is this different from showing up in ChatGPT?

The core idea is the same: clear, crawlable, trustworthy pages. The details differ. ChatGPT uses OpenAI's own search crawler and third-party search providers, so your robots.txt and your Bing presence matter more. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need special markup to appear in AI Overviews?

No. Google says there are no additional technical requirements. A page needs to be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Accurate structured data still helps Google understand your pages.

### Does llms.txt help with Google AI Overviews?

Google says you do not need llms.txt or other special files for its AI features. It does no harm, but it is not a ranking factor for Google.

### Can a small business appear in AI Overviews?

Yes. AI Overviews often cite smaller sites that answer a specific question clearly, especially for local and niche topics where large sites have little to say.

### How do I see if my site appears in AI Overviews?

Search the questions your customers ask and look at the cited links. In Search Console, AI Overview traffic is included in the Performance report, so watch impressions and clicks for those queries over time.
