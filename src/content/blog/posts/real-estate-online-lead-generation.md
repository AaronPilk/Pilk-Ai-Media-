Online real estate leads have a reputation problem. Agents buy them, call them, hear "I'm just looking," and conclude online leads do not work. The truth is that most online leads are early in their journey, and the agents who win with them have better offers and better follow-up, not better luck. Here is what works now.

## What are the main online lead sources for agents?

| Source | Lead type | Cost | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home valuation offers | Sellers | Ads or organic | Weeks |
| Listing pages and video | Buyers and future sellers | Ads or organic | Days |
| Google Business Profile and reviews | Both | Time | Months |
| Local content and SEO | Both | Time | Months |
| Social media content | Both | Time | Months |
| Retargeting ads | Warm visitors | Ad spend | Days |
| Portal leads | Mostly buyers | Paid per lead or share | Immediate |

## How do you generate seller leads online?

Seller leads are the most valuable and hardest to get. What works:

- **Home valuation pages**: "What's your home worth?" with a short form, followed by a real, personal valuation, not just an automated estimate. See [home valuation landing pages](/blog/home-valuation-landing-page/).
- **Just-sold content** showing your results in specific neighborhoods.
- **Market updates** that answer "is now a good time to sell?" for your area.
- **Listing marketing as proof**: every listing campaign shows future sellers how you would market their home.

## How do you generate buyer leads online?

- **Listing pages** with video, photos and neighborhood details, promoted locally. A [single property website](/blog/single-property-websites/) for each listing gives every campaign a professional home.
- **Neighborhood guides** for people relocating or moving up.
- **Open house promotion** with an online sign-up.
- **IDX search**, if you will follow up on registrations. See [IDX websites for realtors](/blog/idx-website-for-realtors/).

## Do social media ads work for real estate?

Yes, within Meta's housing rules. Housing ads cannot target by age, gender or ZIP code and must cover at least a 15-mile radius, so your creative and offer do the targeting. Listing videos, valuation offers and neighborhood content perform best. See [Facebook ads for real estate agents](/blog/facebook-ads-for-real-estate-agents/).

## Are Zillow and portal leads worth it?

They can be, depending on your market, budget and follow-up system. Pay-per-lead and referral-fee models both cut into your margin, and you compete with other agents for the same people. We weigh it up in [are Zillow leads worth it](/blog/are-zillow-leads-worth-it/).

## Why does follow-up decide everything?

Most online leads are months away from a transaction. Agents who give up after two calls lose them to agents who keep showing up. A strong system:

1. **Instant text and email** when a lead arrives.
2. **A call within minutes** during business hours.
3. **A long-term nurture sequence** with listings, market updates and check-ins.
4. **A CRM** that tracks every lead's source, stage and next step.

See [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead/) and [GoHighLevel for real estate](/blog/gohighlevel-for-real-estate/).

## How do you measure online lead generation?

Track by source:

- Cost per lead.
- Percentage who respond.
- Percentage who become appointments.
- Closed deals and commission.

Judge sources over months. A source with expensive leads that close often beats one with cheap leads that never answer.

## Which online lead source should a new agent start with?

Start with what costs time rather than money. Build a simple website with a valuation page, set up your Google Business Profile, ask every past client and friend who has used you for a review, and post consistent local video on one social platform. Add a small, steady ad budget for listing videos or valuation offers once your follow-up system works. Buying portal leads before you can follow up properly usually wastes money; a handful of well-nurtured leads beats a flood you cannot handle.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best online lead source for real estate agents?

For seller leads, home valuation offers backed by fast personal follow-up. For buyers, listing pages and video promoted locally. The best source is the one you can follow up on consistently.

### Why do online real estate leads not answer?

Many are early in their search and filled out a form on impulse. Instant texts, persistent but respectful follow-up and long-term nurturing turn more of them into conversations.

### How long does it take online leads to convert?

Often months. Some convert within weeks, but many buyers and sellers take six months or more from first inquiry to transaction.

### Should I buy real estate leads?

Bought leads can work if your follow-up is strong and the cost fits your margins. Build your own sources too, so you are not dependent on one vendor.
