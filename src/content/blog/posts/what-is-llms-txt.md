If you have been reading about AI search, you have probably seen llms.txt described as the next robots.txt. It is a real proposal and some sites publish one, including ours. It is also widely oversold. Here is what it is, what it does and does not do, and whether your business should add one.

## What is an llms.txt file?

llms.txt is a proposed standard for a Markdown file at the root of a website, such as yourdomain.com/llms.txt. The idea, published in 2024, is to give large language models a clean, compact summary of a site so they do not have to wade through navigation, scripts and layout to understand it.

A typical llms.txt file includes:

- **The site or business name** as a heading.
- **A short summary** of what the business does.
- **Sections of links** to the most important pages, each with a one-line description.
- **Optional details** such as contact information or policies.

## What does an llms.txt file look like?

A simplified example for a local business:

```
# Bayside Plumbing

> Licensed plumbing company serving St. Petersburg and Clearwater, FL. Repairs, water heaters and repiping.

## Services
- [Water heater repair](https://example.com/water-heaters/): repair and replacement
- [Repiping](https://example.com/repiping/): whole-home repiping

## Company
- [About](https://example.com/about/): licensed since 2010
- [Contact](https://example.com/contact/): phone, hours and service area
```

## Do AI tools actually use llms.txt?

This is where the hype outruns the facts. As of late 2026:

- **Google** has said you do not need llms.txt or other special files for its AI features, including AI Overviews and AI Mode. See [how to show up in Google AI Overviews](/blog/how-to-show-up-in-google-ai-overviews/).
- **Major AI search tools** have not said they rely on llms.txt to choose what to cite.
- **Some developer tools and AI coding assistants** do read llms.txt files, especially for software documentation, which is where the format is most useful.

So llms.txt will not move your search rankings or guarantee AI citations.

## Is llms.txt the same as robots.txt?

No. robots.txt tells crawlers what they may and may not crawl, and it is widely respected. llms.txt is a summary for reading, not a set of permissions. If you want to control AI crawlers, use robots.txt rules for specific bots. For example, to appear in ChatGPT search you need to allow OAI-SearchBot. See [how to show up in ChatGPT search](/blog/how-to-show-up-in-chatgpt-search/).

## Should a small business add an llms.txt file?

It is reasonable to add one if:

- It takes your developer a few minutes.
- You keep it accurate when services or prices change.
- You do not expect it to drive traffic.

It is not worth paying much for, and it should never come before the basics that actually help: clear pages that answer questions, accurate business information, reviews, and a site search engines can crawl and index.

## What helps AI tools understand your business more than llms.txt?

1. **Clear, specific pages** for each service, with prices or ranges, areas served and FAQs.
2. **Consistent business details** across your website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places and directories. See [Bing Places for Business](/blog/bing-places-for-business/).
3. **Structured data** such as Organization and LocalBusiness markup that matches the page.
4. **Named authors and dates** on helpful content.
5. **Mentions and reviews** on trusted third-party sites.

These help in regular search and AI search at the same time.

## How do you keep an llms.txt file accurate?

If you add one, treat it like any other page: update it when you add services, change prices or move pages. An outdated llms.txt is worse than none, because it gives AI tools wrong information. On our own site, the file is part of the codebase and updated with the pages it points to.

## What is the difference between llms.txt and llms-full.txt?

Some sites publish a second file, often called llms-full.txt, containing the full text of key pages in one document, mainly for software documentation that developers feed into AI tools. For a small business site, a short llms.txt is plenty if you publish one at all. Your actual pages, written clearly, remain what search engines and AI tools read. Spend your effort there first, and treat these files as a tidy extra rather than a strategy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does llms.txt improve SEO?

No evidence shows it affects Google rankings, and Google says it is not needed for its AI features. It is a small optional extra.

### Where do I put an llms.txt file?

At the root of your domain, so it loads at yourdomain.com/llms.txt, written in Markdown.

### Can llms.txt block AI crawlers?

No. Use robots.txt rules for specific crawlers to control access. llms.txt only summarizes your site.

### Is llms.txt an official standard?

It is a community proposal, not a formal standard adopted by search engines or AI companies.
