The build gets all the attention, but maintenance decides whether your website stays fast, secure and accurate for years. Owners often underestimate it, or pay for maintenance plans that do little. This guide explains what maintenance actually involves, what drives the cost and how to choose.

## What does website maintenance include?

Good maintenance covers five areas:

1. **Hosting and uptime**: keeping the site online and fast, with monitoring that alerts someone if it goes down.
2. **Security**: software and plugin updates, SSL certificates and protection against attacks.
3. **Backups**: regular copies of the site and a tested way to restore them.
4. **Content updates**: new prices, services, team members, photos and pages.
5. **Health checks**: broken links, forms that stopped sending, slow pages and search console errors.

Plans that cover only hosting and "updates" without edits or checks often leave the most important work undone.

## What drives the cost of maintaining a website?

| Factor | Lower cost | Higher cost |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Static site or hosted builder | Self-hosted WordPress with many plugins |
| Change frequency | Rare updates | Weekly changes |
| Features | Brochure site with forms | Bookings, ecommerce, member areas, integrations |
| Traffic and risk | Low traffic, no payments | High traffic, payments or sensitive data |
| Who does it | You | A developer or agency |

## Why do WordPress sites cost more to maintain?

WordPress itself is solid, but most sites depend on themes and plugins from many different developers. Each needs updating, updates can conflict, and outdated plugins are a common way sites get hacked. That means regular updates, testing and backups. It is manageable, but it is real work and someone has to own it.

## Why do static sites cost less to maintain?

A static site is a set of prebuilt files served from a fast network, with no database or admin login on the server. There is much less to update and much less to attack. Most of the ongoing cost is content edits. This is how we build most sites, including our own, and it is why our maintenance conversations focus on edits and improvements rather than patching.

## Can you maintain your own website?

Yes, if:

- Your platform makes edits easy, such as a website builder.
- You will actually check it monthly.
- Someone knows how to restore a backup if something breaks.

A simple monthly routine: test every form, click through main pages on a phone, check Google Search Console for errors, update anything outdated, and confirm backups ran. Our guide to [Google Search Console for beginners](/blog/google-search-console-for-beginners/) explains what to look for.

## What happens if you skip maintenance?

The problems build quietly:

- Forms stop sending leads and nobody notices for weeks.
- Old prices and services confuse customers.
- Outdated software gets exploited.
- Pages slow down and search rankings slip.
- An expired domain or SSL certificate takes the site offline.

We have seen owners lose months of leads to a broken form. That alone usually costs more than a year of maintenance. If you suspect something is off, start with [why a website does not generate leads](/blog/why-is-my-website-not-generating-leads/).

## How do you choose a maintenance plan?

Ask:

1. What exactly is included each month, including edit time?
2. How quickly do you respond to problems?
3. How often are backups made, and have restores been tested?
4. Who owns the hosting account and domain?
5. Can I cancel and take my site with me?

## What should a monthly maintenance report include?

If you pay for maintenance, ask for a short monthly report: uptime, updates applied, backups confirmed, forms tested, edits made, and any issues found and fixed. It keeps the work visible and makes it easy to tell whether the plan is worth what you pay.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is website maintenance necessary?

Yes. At minimum, someone needs to keep the site online, secure, backed up and accurate, and check that forms still deliver leads.

### How often should a website be updated?

Check it monthly and update content whenever prices, services or contact details change. Security updates should be applied promptly.

### Does a website builder include maintenance?

Builders like Wix and Squarespace handle hosting, security and software updates. You still maintain your own content and check that forms and integrations work.

### What is the difference between hosting and maintenance?

Hosting keeps the site online. Maintenance keeps it secure, backed up, accurate and working. Many plans combine both.
