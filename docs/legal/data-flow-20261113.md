# pilk.ai privacy and data-flow audit, 13 Nov 2026

What the site actually does with visitor and client data, checked against the
code on branch `claude/seo-foundation-20261113` (base `7b44c60`, the commit live
on pilk.ai on 13 Nov 2026) and against the live site with curl. The Privacy
Policy (`/privacy/`) and Terms of Service (`/terms/`) are written to match this
file. If the code changes, update this file and the two policies together.

How it was checked:

- read every form component, both form schemas, `functions/api/*`,
  `wrangler.toml`, `public/_headers`, `.env.example` and the layout
- grepped `src/`, `public/` and the built `out/` for third-party hosts, cookies,
  `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, analytics and pixel code
- fetched `/`, `/contact/`, `/mortgage/`, `/checkout/`, `/inquiry/`,
  `/privacy/`, `/terms/` and a demo template from pilk.ai and listed every
  external host and every `Set-Cookie` header, once with curl's default
  headers and once with a browser `Accept`/`User-Agent` (Cloudflare only
  injects some scripts into browser requests)
- loaded the live home page and a local build in headless Chromium
  (Playwright) and recorded every third-party request and cookie

**Update, same day (lead-capture and consent change):** sections 1, 2, 3 and 5
below were revised after `/api/leads` was changed to validate each form by
its own `formType`, the optional SMS consent box was added, and the checkout
got an auto-renewal disclosure with a required box. The original findings are
kept where they explain why.

**Update, same day (quote-only release, owner instruction 13 Nov 2026 14:49
ET):** pilk.ai no longer sells online. Every price is a "Starting at" figure,
`/api/checkout` answers 410 and never calls Stripe, `/checkout/` and its
success and cancel pages are noindex stubs that forward to `/contact/`, the
template order became a template *request* (a quote request, nothing charged),
GoHighLevel now also gets tags added without replacing, custom fields, an
opportunity in the PILK AI pipeline and a note on every lead, and the lead
endpoints limit accepted submissions per IP. Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 are
revised for this; the earlier text is kept where it explains why.

Status words: **Active** = runs on the live site today. **Dormant** = code
exists but the configuration it needs is not in the repo, so it does not run
unless the owner set a secret in Cloudflare that this audit cannot see.

## 3. Payments

**Since the quote-only release, pilk.ai takes no payments.** Every project
starts with a written proposal or quote; work starts once it is approved and
the invoice (or deposit) is paid. Stripe stays the processor for invoices and
payment links the owner sends, which are paid on Stripe's own pages.

- `/api/checkout` answers 410 (`checkout_retired`) to any request and never
  calls Stripe. `/checkout/`, `/checkout/success/` and `/checkout/cancel/` are
  noindex stubs with a meta refresh and a link to `/contact/`.
- The checkout UI, its add-on price table, the 10% fees & processing line and
  the renewal box are removed. `src/lib/checkout-pricing.ts` keeps only what
  the webhook needs.
- `functions/api/stripe-webhook.ts` is deployed **unchanged**, so hosting plans
  started at the former checkout keep working: their subscription, the order
  and plan-terms emails and the cancellation link. The Terms keep a paragraph
  for those plans with the wording their buyers ticked.
- The 10% fee drip-pricing finding in section 7 is resolved: no fee is
  advertised or charged on the site.

What the former checkout did (kept for the record):

- **Processor: Stripe.** `functions/api/checkout.ts` creates a Stripe Checkout
  Session (`mode: payment`) for the mortgage website: $500 build, $100 per
  add-on (rush, GoHighLevel connection, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, PostHog),
  plus a 10% "Fees & processing" line on the whole order. Prices are recomputed
  on the server from `src/lib/checkout-pricing.ts`.
- `customer_creation: "always"` and
  `payment_intent_data.setup_future_usage: "off_session"`: Stripe creates a
  customer and saves the card for later charges. `allow_promotion_codes: true`.
  `billing_address_collection: "auto"`.
- `functions/api/stripe-webhook.ts` verifies Stripe's signature, then creates a
  **$100/month hosting subscription with a 30-day trial**, so the first monthly
  charge lands 30 days after the build payment. It renews monthly until
  cancelled. There is no self-serve cancel button in the code.
- pilk.ai stores no card data. The browser is sent to Stripe's hosted page;
  the function only sees the session, the Stripe customer ID, the payment
  intent and payment method IDs, the customer email and the amount. The
  checkout UI says "Card details never touch our servers", which is accurate.
- **Auto-renewal disclosure (added in this change).** Before the pay button the
  order summary shows the 10% fees & processing line with a note that it
  applies only to today's one-time order, then a "renews automatically" box:
  what renews, $100 per month, the first charge date (computed in the buyer's
  browser) and that it is 30 days after today, monthly until cancelled, and
  how to cancel (email Aaron@pilk.ai through a pre-addressed link, or call;
  same as the Terms). A required, unticked box reads "I agree to the
  $100/month hosting plan starting 30 days after purchase, renewing monthly
  until I cancel." The page won't call `/api/checkout` until it is ticked, and
  `/api/checkout` answers 400 without `renewalConsent: true` and the current
  wording version (`RENEWAL_CONSENT_VERSION`), before it touches Stripe.
- The Checkout Session metadata carries `renewal_consent`,
  `renewal_consent_version`, `renewal_consent_at` and `renewal_terms`; the
  webhook copies the first three onto the subscription's metadata.
- The template order's "what happens next" now states the 10% charge and the
  monthly plan before the visitor reaches checkout. The mortgage page still
  says "$500 flat" (left for the owner's fee decision, see section 7).

## 4. Cookies, browser storage, analytics and pixels

**Main site (all Next.js pages):** one analytics tool runs, no pixels.

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics: Active.** Cloudflare's automatic setup injects
  `static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js` (token
  `46641eb31a9e472e9fd0cac949f88d2b`) into every HTML page at the edge. It is
  configured in the Cloudflare dashboard, not in this repo, and it only shows up
  for browser-like requests, so a plain `curl` misses it. It sets no cookies and
  uses no local storage; Cloudflare receives the page URL, referrer, user agent
  and IP (for country) plus page-timing data. The new demo-page CSP (below)
  blocks it on `/template-previews/*`.
- `src/components/layout/Analytics.tsx` can load GA4 and the Meta Pixel when
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID` are set, but the component
  is not rendered anywhere, so it never loads. `trackEvent()` only dispatches a
  local browser event and calls `gtag`/`fbq` if they exist, which they don't.
- No `Set-Cookie` on any page checked, and no `document.cookie` in the code.
  Cloudflare can set its own security cookies (`__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`) if it
  challenges a visitor or runs bot detection.
- Fonts (Space Grotesk, Inter) are self-hosted by `next/font` at build time; no
  request goes to Google Fonts from the main site.
- Cloudflare adds `/cdn-cgi/scripts/.../email-decode.min.js` (email address
  obfuscation, served from pilk.ai) and a Network Error Logging header
  (`report-to`/`nel`, `success_fraction: 0`: the browser reports failed
  requests only, to Cloudflare).

Browser storage the site writes:

| Key | Storage | Contents | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pilk-theme` | localStorage | "light" or "dark" | until cleared |
| `pilk-intro-seen` | sessionStorage | "1" after the intro animation plays | tab session |
| `pilk-attribution` | sessionStorage | UTM values, landing page, referrer, gclid, fbclid | tab session |
| `pilk-project-brief-draft` | localStorage | every text field typed into the project brief (name, email, phone, company, notes...). Files are not saved | until the brief is submitted (then removed) or storage is cleared |
| `pilk_intake_v2` | localStorage | every text field typed into the template intake and its links | until submitted, "Start over", or cleared |

**Demo template sites (`/template-previews/*`, 131 HTML files):** every file
contains a Google tag snippet with the placeholder ID `G-XXXXXXXXXX` and a Meta
Pixel snippet with the placeholder ID `000000000000000`, plus Google Fonts.
The home page, `/templates/`, each `/templates/<slug>/` page and `/mortgage/`
embed live previews of these demos in same-origin iframes, so this is not
limited to people who open a demo. Measured on the live site on 13 Nov 2026
(Playwright, headless Chromium): one home page visit loaded `gtag.js` 9 times
and `fbevents.js` 9 times, sent 9 hits to `www.google-analytics.com/g/collect`
with the placeholder ID, and set the `_ga` and `_ga_XXXXXXXXXX` cookies on
pilk.ai. Meta's script also queues a PageView for the placeholder pixel and can
set `_fbp`. That is Google Analytics and an ad pixel collecting data on pilk.ai
with no business purpose and no disclosure.

**Fixed in this change:** `public/_headers` now sends a Content-Security-Policy
for `/template-previews/*` that allows only pilk.ai scripts plus Google Fonts.
The browser blocks the Google tag, the Meta Pixel, the noscript pixel image and
the injected Cloudflare beacon. Re-measured on a local `wrangler pages dev` of
this branch: on the home page, a template page and a demo page, every
`googletagmanager.com` and `connect.facebook.net` request fails with `csp`, no
Google Analytics hit is sent and no cookie is set.
The template files themselves are untouched, so they can still be duplicated
for clients with real IDs. Google Fonts still load on the demo pages, so Google
receives the visitor's IP address, browser details and the demo page as
referrer when the fonts are fetched. The Privacy Policy says so.

With that fix there is no sale of personal information and no sharing for
cross-context behavioural advertising anywhere on pilk.ai. Until the branch is
deployed, the live site still has the problem above.

## 5. SMS, uploads and embeds

- **SMS:** the site sends no texts itself; there is no SMS code in the repo.
  Phone numbers are collected (optional) on every lead form and go to
  GoHighLevel, which can text. Since this change the brief (step 1), the
  contact form, the quick inquiry and the template request have an optional,
  unticked SMS consent box directly under the phone field, shown once a number
  is typed and cleared if the number is removed. Its wording lives in
  `src/lib/sms-consent.ts` under a version key (`sms-v1-2026-11-13`); the
  component renders that same string and the server records the version and
  the full text. Consent is recorded only when the box is ticked, the phone has
  7 to 15 digits and the version is a published one. The template request's
  required box doesn't mention texting. The listing demo forms have no SMS
  box. When consent is valid, GHL also gets the wording version and the time
  in two contact fields, which a later form without consent never clears.
- **File uploads:** yes, on `/contact/` and `/start/<template>/` (limits in
  section 1). Files travel through the Pages Function to Resend as email
  attachments and land in the business mailbox. R2 storage is dormant.
- **Embeds:** none. Videos are self-hosted MP4s. No YouTube, Vimeo, Calendly,
  chat widget, map embed, reCAPTCHA or Turnstile (a Turnstile key name is in
  `.env.example` but no code uses it). Template previews are same-origin
  iframes. Outbound links go to client sites, profile sites and Placester's
  pricing page.

## 7. Other findings for the owner

- **Advertised price vs checkout total. Resolved in the quote-only release:**
  there is no checkout, no fee line and no "flat" price; the mortgage page says
  "Starting at $500". Original finding: the mortgage page said "$500 flat";
  checkout adds a mandatory 10% "Fees & processing" line ($50 on the base
  build). Advertising a price that leaves out a mandatory fee is drip pricing
  (FTC Act; California's SB 478 for California buyers), and a 10% card fee is
  far above the card networks' surcharge cap. Either fold the fee into the
  advertised price or drop it. The Terms disclose it as it stands.
- **Own vs rent price.** It said "From $750 (template)"; `packages.ts` says
  the Complete Website is $1,000. Fixed to read from `packages.ts`.
- **CAN-SPAM.** Marketing emails (for example from GoHighLevel) must carry a
  valid postal address. A PO box or mailbox service keeps the home address
  private.
- **Dormant integrations.** If `SUPABASE_URL`/`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` or
  `CRM_WEBHOOK_URL` are set as Pages secrets, that provider must be added to
  the Privacy Policy's provider list. If they are not, the dead code paths can
  be removed.
  The deploy kit now prints the names (never the values) of the Pages
  project's secrets before deploying, so this can be checked. If Supabase is
  on, its rows keep the brief's field names for every form and do not include
  the SMS consent record (that lives in the notification email and the GHL
  note); `CRM_WEBHOOK_URL` gets the record as `smsConsent`.
- **GoHighLevel tags are replaced on every upsert. Fixed in the quote-only
  release** (the owner asked for the GHL setup): the upsert no longer carries
  tags and tags are added with the Add Tags endpoint, so nothing is removed.
  Workflows that trigger on "tag added" see the same tag names as before.
  Original finding: a contact who sends a
  second form, or pays, loses the tags from the first (`pilk-paid` replaces
  `pilk-website-lead`, and a later form without the SMS box ticked drops
